# Modeler experiment — results, 2026-09-26

Question: can an AI runtime author, writing and revising Blender construction programs from a few catalog photos,
produce a live-try-on GLB that resembles the product better than the fixed constructions of the previous routes,
through a generic unattended job? Code, protocol and policies: `automation/modeler/` (README.md, DESIGN.md).

## What was built and verified

* A generic job (`python -m modeler.job --request ... --output ...`): intake (mattes, lens outlines, mm measurements),
  author turns, a fresh Blender 5.2 process per candidate, export through the existing contract writer and checker,
  host-owned camera fitting and metrics, EEVEE observation sheets, the actual TryOnRenderer through the existing
  harness, an incumbent rule, an independent evaluator, explicit statuses and a manifest naming the exact GLB.
* Author drivers: `astra` (gpt-6-astra, Responses API, three strict tools, one call per turn, call ledger of 1-10
  per authorization plus a cumulative dollar ledger with pre-call worst-case estimates), `package` (fresh agents
  answering recorded turn packages, transcripts audited), `scripted` (replay).
* 32 unit and Blender tests pass (`python -m unittest discover -s tests -p "test_modeler_*.py"`), including the
  Blender camera reproducing the host camera model to within one pixel and the Astra driver over a mocked session.
* Clean-loop evidence per job: `journal.jsonl`, `turns/turn-NNNN/` (request, decision, API receipts or agent
  transcript audit), `candidates/cNNNN/` (program modules with hashes, build, `candidate.blend`, GLB, observations,
  held-out measurements), `evaluation/`, `manifest.json`, `REPORT.md`.

## Owner verdicts (live AR try-on, 2026-09-26)

The owner tried both Astra-authored assets on their own face in the AR app (`python -m modeler.tryon`, Chrome, own
camera) and judged each "perfect": `vb-astra1` c0004 and `miu-astra2` c0002. Recorded with `modeler.owner_verdict`
against the assets' digests (`<job>/owner_verdict.json`, manifests upgraded to `accepted` with `accepted_by: owner`,
the automatic verdicts kept beside them) and appended to `data/modeler/calibration/owner_verdicts.jsonl`.
`vb-run1` c0003 was not judged.

What these two points say about the automatic bar (both rows are owner-accepts, so they bound the bar from one side
only):

* The Astra evaluator's `reject` on VB with 5 majors (planar section, endpiece slab, grey EEVEE materials, opaque
  preview lenses, asset-back panel) was a false reject: three of its five majors describe the EEVEE preview or the
  harness, not the asset the owner saw in the runtime, and the two geometric ones did not matter on a face at
  mirror distance. Evaluator judgements must weigh the AR panels, not the preview sheets, and the bar is coarser than
  silhouette detail at the 0.1 mm level.
* The provisional held-out threshold (1.2 mm) rejected an asset the owner accepted at 1.24 mm; the front/lens
  thresholds (0.6 mm) agreed with the owner on both. The held-out threshold is too tight or measures the wrong
  thing (temple depth at a nominal scale) for the live impression.
* Two accepts and zero rejects cannot calibrate a bar; the owner then judged the rest of the try-on page in the same

### Lens colour: diagnosis, three capabilities, two reruns (2026-09-26, night)

The owner's note on both shields ("lens colour close but not right") measured as: Invu the right hue at 0.64 of the
photo's brightness and 1.8x its saturation; Oakley olive instead of mint (hue error 0.28), 0.68 of the brightness,
no pink-to-green shift. Causes: the helpers exposed one head-on `mirror_rgb` while the runtime's descriptor supports
an angular reflectance table (the colour shift was inexpressible); the runtime lights a mirror with its dim neutral
room where the catalog shot used a bright studio; the author had no colour measurement at all. Built (DESIGN.md,
"Lens colour"): `gl.lens_optics(mirror_angular=...)` exported to the runtime's angular table; `modeler/lens_colour.py`
(lens core of the actual-AR front render, found by projecting the lens meshes with the harness's recorded matrices,
vs the photo's lens core: hue_error, saturation_ratio, value_ratio in `summary.lens_colour`, shown to the author each
turn and to the evaluator); `lens_env_intensity_recommended` (photo/render brightness ratio for mirrored lenses, in
the manifest handover and the try-on link as `lensenv`); `--seed-program` (a job starts from a previous candidate's
program as its own measured first candidate). Verified by a seeded dry run and 44 tests.

## What the loop demonstrably did (from the journals)

* Astra on VB: targeted repairs by module (11 turns: 2 view requests, 7 submissions inheriting unchanged modules
  byte-identical, 2 rollbacks to the valid incumbent after Boolean constructions failed the contract); it diagnosed
  its own build error from the traceback (turn 1) and a HOST defect from the export notes (miu-astra1 turn 3).
* The agent author on VB reasoned from the side outlines to change the wrap radius and add hinge blocks (turn 1)
  and found a sign error in its own endpiece slope from the overlay (turn 2).
* Both authors reached AR-compatible assets on the first or second build; every later valid candidate loaded in the
  actual renderer with two optical meshes and no continuity failure (the fixture BSA assets fail continuity).
* The agent author's five VB candidates all built, passed the contract and loaded in AR; three of five improved the
  incumbent (0.62 -> 0.48 -> 0.43 -> 0.42 mm), the fifth regressed and was not delivered. Its turns took 10-58 min
  each; one attempt died on an output-token overrun and one was stopped by the developer for a contaminated
  dispatcher prompt (recorded in turns/turn-0003).

## Interventions (all generic host fixes, none item-specific; full table in DESIGN.md)

Evidence keys inside Blender; response-file stability; phantom charges for refused calls (ledger corrected with
reversal rows); unsent attempts blocking a fresh attempt; clay lighting; degenerate triangles (drop only from open
objects) and n-gon fan triangulation; lens energy rule capping instead of raising; visible-lens metric; topology
counts surfaced to authors. miu-astra1 was stopped because its process had loaded a host exporter that opened closed
parts; the author had correctly diagnosed this; the job was restarted as miu-astra2 on the fixed code.

## Diagnosis of the main remaining defects

* VB rim profile and endpiece: representation/construction. Both authors build the front as a bevelled plate plus
  blocks; a convex moulded section needs a sweep of a rounded profile along the outline (`gl.rim_ring` exists but
  the authors did not use it) or subdivision with creases. Astra's attempts at rounder sections failed the contract
  twice (Boolean unions producing non-manifold geometry) and it fell back to the valid incumbent.
* VB back/held-out silhouettes: camera/shape coupling — the wrap radius and endpiece depth are inferred from two
  side photos at unknown scale; the held-out angled view is where a wrong depth shows.
* Miu lens edge: evidence extraction — the measured rimless outline is noisy where the temple tips and mounts
  overlap the clear lens; the author used it verbatim.
* Materials: the EEVEE preview desaturates the acetate and the lenses render too opaque, so material judgments
  must come from the AR sheet; the runtime shows the navy and the gradient correctly.

## Limits of this evidence

* No physical dimension was supplied: every millimetre is nominal (140 mm front width assumed, ±10 %).
* The automatic visual bar is not calibrated: no job awarded `accepted` on its own. The two `accepted` statuses are
  owner verdicts in the live try-on (above); `best_effort` means a valid, measured, independently evaluated asset
  with recorded defects and no owner verdict.
* Two development products only; both were used to tune BSA earlier, though nothing from BSA enters these jobs
  (no donor meshes, no solved assets; provenance in each manifest). Miu was not used to tune the modeler runtime
  before its run, apart from the generic fixes listed above.
* AR checks use the harness's synthetic canonical face; no real-person or device test was run.
* The Astra jobs stopped at the 10-call authorization or at the account's credit limit, not at convergence.
