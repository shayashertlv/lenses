## What exists and what it establishes

1. A resumable job flow: immutable photo intake/normalization, existing-model import or explicit provider initialization, shared geometry refinement, region observations and optional optical preparation/fitting. Saved jobs verify and reuse artifacts.
2. Shared multiview geometry refinement that preserves asset attributes and checks exported geometry. It does not invent missing components or infer temple articulation. A stated `frame_width` is applied by a `scale` stage before refinement (positions to metres across the X extent); the other dimensions are recorded only.
3. Canonical lens appearance definitions and native/export/browser paths for absorption tint, optical-density gradients and separate angular/mirror reflection. Actual AR integration exists, including experimental closed/multipart optical groups. Rendering defined materials is much further along than inferring them from photos.
4. Complete source-component inventory, reversible face partition export and verified physical-group declaration bindings. These preserve all source faces; supplied group hypotheses are not automatically correct semantic labels.
5. Independent image-only coarse lens/frame predictions and SAM alternatives, complete component-to-aperture projections, and a bounded shared cross-view support optimizer.

**The objective is not achieved. There is no independently accepted, fully automatic photos-to-AR reconstruction.** Candidates remain `accepted=false` / `quality_verdict=unmeasured`. High projected overlap, successful export and passing software tests are not model-quality proof.
