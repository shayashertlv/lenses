# Automatic articulated geometry

The default `refine_photos.run` now separates photo-specific arm opening from the shared rest asset. It no longer requires caller-supplied face roles or hinges. This is a bounded automatic hypothesis generator and fitter, not a claim that arbitrary product photos determine an exact 3D model.

## Production path

1. `automatic_articulation.infer_automatic_part_bindings` inventories connected source surfaces within each primitive, finds lateral depth-dominant arm cores, joins nearby fragments, and checks actual arm/front surface attachment. Names, lens color, and product identity are not inputs. The current mechanical prior is +Y hinges in the pipeline's +Y-up/+Z-front coordinates. Unsupported fused, detached, or already-folded source geometry explicitly abstains.
2. `refine_photos._automatic_articulated_run` seeds each camera from measured contrast or authored-alpha geometry, then fits the front camera using only source-bound front contour correspondences. `fit_photo_arm_states` fits each arm independently against image edges outside the front projection. Fixed spatial holdout cells, minimum support, visibility retention, and a minimum holdout gain are required. A retained pose must also have mean holdout error at most 1.5% of projected object width, with at least 80% of points within 1.2% width; a 0.5-pixel floor accommodates raster quantization. Relative improvement alone cannot pass a poor match. Invisible or ambiguous arms retain rest pose.
3. `structured_refinement.fit_shared_geometry` can improve the one rest asset after photo poses are fixed. The existing verified GLB deformation export is used. Reloaded float32 geometry must preserve source face lineage and pass actual front contour and frozen-arm nonregression in the photos. Failed exports stay rejected artifacts.
4. `view_scene.make_view_scene_contract` records source bytes and numeric geometry hashes, complete source-face roles, hinges, per-photo pose and photo hashes. `pose_mesh_from_contract` is shared by component projection, region projection, composition, optical ray fields, and the physical-group caller. Target triangle order is preserved; UVs/normals follow duplicated hinge vertices. New or ambiguously matched triangles remain static/unresolved.
5. `candidate_cameras.transfer_cameras_to_partition` rebinds the same contract through exact source-face ranges in a verified partition receipt. It does not assume preserved node order or global triangle ordinals. This is necessary when source pieces are split, reordered or later have interior walls removed.

Authored alpha supplies geometry support only. Hidden RGB is excluded from geometry edge construction; alpha is not interpreted as a photographed background or lens transmission.

## Camera support repair under evaluation

The bounded camera-bank experiment found that the Miu back photograph already has a useful whole-scene camera: pitch 12.25 degrees, both rest arms absolutely supported. The front-only camera moved to 33.31 degrees and lost both arms for only 0.227 pixels of mean front improvement; its front p95 error worsened. The corresponding Oakley control retains one supported arm and improves its front fit, so a blanket ban on front-only updates would discard useful evidence.

`qa/staged_camera_guard/camera-support-guard.patch` stages a generic repair outside production while a separate immutable job is running. It fits front cameras on a fixed training split, evaluates both coarse and refined hypotheses on identical front witnesses, and fits arms under both cameras. It retains the coarse hypothesis when a refined camera loses absolute arm support without a front improvement beyond `max(0.5 pixels, 0.005 * projected width)`. A front mean or p95 regression beyond that tolerance is independently vetoed. Arm support is counted across sides; a legitimate side-visibility swap does not cause rejection. These are internal witness checks, not independent product validation, because initialization already used the photo.

Two isolated-process probes execute the staged refinement implementation without changing production source. In `miu-camera-guard-staged-v1`, the back update's true held-out mean improves only 1.041 to 0.923 pixels while p95 worsens 1.852 to 2.300 and absolute arm support falls 2 to 0. In `oakley-camera-guard-staged-v1`, front and back camera updates remain retained: back front-witness mean improves 0.764 to 0.714 pixels, p95 improves 1.740 to 1.387, and supported arm count remains one. ## Remaining limits

- Hinge axes and roles remain geometry/manufacturing hypotheses; the detector can abstain on fused or badly oriented provider geometry. It does not infer arbitrary mechanical joint topology.
- Photographic edges outside the front can include studio shadows or unrelated objects. Spatial holdout reduces overfitting but does not establish semantic edge identity or ground-truth pose.
- The camera still uses a weak-perspective/perspective family and can absorb errors in the initial shape. Existing product photos do not guarantee unique camera/shape recovery.
- An unseen arm remains unchanged; its shape and fold angle are not invented. Shared shape changes require measured front evidence and frozen arm nonregression, but unseen surface detail remains inherited from initialization.
- Triangle correspondence abstains on ambiguous coincident surfaces. Exact partition receipts carry roles where geometry alone would be ambiguous.

## Whole-frame evidence coverage

`frame_surface_coverage.assess_frame_surface_coverage` measures area over all non-optical triangles, including untextured or unmapped frame surfaces. Every triangle has 16 equal-area barycentric cells; covered cells require samples near their corners and center, each seen in three mutually separated cameras. Duplicate atlas texels cannot inflate the denominator or observed area. Six tests verify full/tiny-subset coverage, missing data, angular degeneracy and duplicate-density invariance. Finite sampling does not prove visibility between samples; the delivery report records that limitation.

## Independent delivery measurements

`production_validation.measure_heldout_views` can measure new photographs against the frozen candidate. Its receipt records the exact candidate, photograph inputs, complete current-job multiview intake receipts and camera-fitting resolution. Training-image exclusion compares both source bytes and normalized decoded pixels, so a re-encoded training photo cannot become a held-out view. `run_delivery_validation` remeasures supported receipts rather than trusting their recorded scores. The trusted current-job history is provided separately by production orchestration; a caller's `source_history_complete=True` assertion is insufficient.

Delivery validation takes `measurement_receipts.heldout = {"path": ..., "sha256": ...}` and requires the receipt's model hash and intake references to match this delivery. Scalar-only `heldout`, `appearance` or `required_parts_present` wrappers cannot grant acceptance. The pure `evaluate_acceptance` function remains usable for threshold controls, including known wrong-hue, reversed-gradient, opaque-lens and missing-arm cases. The conditional visible-part producer described below now supplies replayable extent evidence; it does not establish semantic identity. Physical-appearance evidence still requires its separate calibrated controls. Eight original validation tests cover wrong-asset receipts, forged scores and decoded-image reuse.

## Required visible-part measurement

`required_parts.run_required_parts_stage(final_model, refinement_reference, region_reference, output)` now constructs replayable support recipes and a final-candidate measurement. `replay_required_parts` requires the current job's trusted refinement and region references; saved scalar results are ignored. The measurement establishes conditional image-supported extent, not semantic identity or independent held-out accuracy.

Final triangles receive roles only through exact source-corner multiset correspondence. Removed interior triangles are allowed; added copies exceeding source multiplicity, conflicting coincident roles and unmatched LOD triangles remain unmeasured. Full-scene first-hit rasterization preserves final face IDs and barycentric coordinates. A source-fixed hinge-to-tip coordinate divides each arm into six bins, so a shortened arm cannot rescale its remaining geometry into a complete arm. Accepted extent needs five bins including both endpoints and at least two useful, angularly separated photographs. One observed edge pixel cannot certify two role samples. Unknown membership and occlusion are unmeasured; a sufficiently visible role over independently known alpha exterior is contradictory evidence.

JPEG support replays the image-only contrast-object region: all five prompt families and all three alternatives, source contrast and independently pinned native pixels. Candidate optical priors can only exclude support. Its consensus is corroboration and may share segmentation errors. Authored alpha is a separate replayable object-support source; neither producer claims physical opacity. A nearby supported interior contour can corroborate an eroded boundary within the fixed distance/normal limits; low-contrast unknown pixels and unrelated gradient lines cannot create positive evidence alone.

Thirteen production tests pass, including a complete JPEG support-to-final-receipt replay, lossless compact geometry, missing/copied arms, coincident conflicting roles, exclusive edge ownership, an unknown background line, full occlusion, incomplete tip extent, source/array tampering and unsupported LOD correspondence.

The real final Oakley compact asset at `data/build-five/oakley-all-views-job-v2/stages/delivery/attempt_1/compact/compact.glb` was measured against all five original JPEG inputs. Its front has all six extent bins in five useful views; its right temple has all six bins in four views. The left temple remains **unmeasured**: only bins 0–4 in one useful view, with no supported tip bin. Overall required-parts status is therefore unmeasured, not a pass. Exact source-to-final correspondence succeeds. See `data/required-parts/oakley-final-v1/measurement/report.json` and its per-view face/barycentric/pixel arrays.
