## Physical-group hypotheses and photographed-ray contents

The new [physical-group stage](PHYSICAL_GROUPS.md) infers competing partitions over every source component from projected co-occupancy and depth agreement at a fixed five-rung tolerance ladder (0.005 to 0.08 of the source extent). Coincident surfaces merge, partial pieces of one surface join through the surface that contains them, separated layers and partial overlaps stay apart, and coincident minor fragments attach to exactly one group without ever bridging two. Role evidence (optical candidate, non-optical, unresolved) is evaluated per aperture branch on the group union and never feeds back into membership. A per-pixel ray-composition map states what each photographed pixel would contain under a hypothesis.

## Architectural findings that change the next step

The historical appearance pipeline has incompatible material definitions across preview, export and AR: different gradient interpolation, weakened mirrored coating, mixed transmitted/reflected colors, and a shadow path that omits exported vertex-color gradients. The detailed source evidence is in `ARCHITECTURE.md`. The canonical paths address these contract differences; the automation does not yet infer and attach those descriptors to arbitrary product assets.

The `LensAppearance` contract remains an effective reference model with a limited production profile. It has verified native/GPU implementations for optical-density gradients, independent strong colored reflection and empirical angular response. Production roughness has directional/relative checks under structured lighting, without a microfacet reference proof. Front/back asymmetry, spatial coating maps, full multilayer physics and automatic inference from product photographs remain unimplemented.
