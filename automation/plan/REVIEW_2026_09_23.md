Review of the glasses reconstruction work — 23 September 2026

The goal for this review is existing product photographs to a model that looks like the product in the actual AR renderer. The user clarified that face-width fitting belongs to the AR engine. Exact physical dimensions and manufacturing accuracy are therefore not primary reconstruction criteria. Relative proportions, recognizable geometry, materials, and a compatible export still matter.

My assessment: preserve the current automation. It is a substantial working research pipeline that produces candidates, but it is not yet a reliable automatic reconstruction product. The next phase should improve the evidence used to estimate geometry and appearance, and establish independent visual evaluation. Another general rewrite or another long sequence of repairs on the same products would be a poor use of the existing work.

**What the current pipeline does.**

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[Existing product photos] --> B[Meshy generation and split]
    B --> C[Transfer textures and normalize export coordinates]
    C --> D[Fit cameras and deform existing mesh]
    D --> E[Infer physical lens groups]
    E --> F[Propose image regions and fit material alternatives]
    F --> G[Choose appearance candidate and export GLB]
    G --> H[Actual AR renderer]
```

The generator supplies most of the shape. Local refinement applies bounded changes to existing topology; it does not reconstruct missing parts. Physical grouping distinguishes potential lens bodies from other geometry. The optical fitter compares tint, gradient and mirror explanations, then the exporter transports the chosen descriptor to AR. Immutable inputs, preserved original generations, stage recovery, geometry lineage, texture transfer and renderer integration are valuable working engineering.

Rendering a defined material is further developed than discovering the correct material from photographs. Successful export, successful rendering, successful optimization, and resemblance to the product are different properties. The current job correctly keeps `accepted=false` and `quality_verdict=unmeasured`.

**The main blockers, in practical terms.**

1. **Component evidence enters too late to guide camera and shape fitting.** Fresh clear-lens models can be rendered as solid footprints and fitted to contrast masks in which the clear lenses barely exist. The opaque-only camera hypothesis depends on already-declared optical materials, while the better physical lens interpretation happens later. See [refine_photos.py:100](C:/Users/Shay/PycharmProjects/lenses/automation/reconstruction/refine_photos.py:100) and [camera.py:89](C:/Users/Shay/PycharmProjects/lenses/automation/reconstruction/camera.py:89). Miu v16 later excludes its far lens from the angled photograph because only 68.2% projects inside its aperture. That protects color fitting from contaminated samples, but leaves the registration problem unresolved. Independent frame/lens/temple observations should inform pose and geometry before optical fitting.

2. **The shape model is too limited to repair substantial generator mistakes.** The shared 4×3×3 deformation cage preserves topology, holds fitted cameras fixed, and bounds movement. It cannot create a missing bridge, separate a fused part, or reliably fix an incorrect rim profile. One static shape also cannot explain existing photographs with differently opened temples. See [deformation.py:432](C:/Users/Shay/PycharmProjects/lenses/automation/reconstruction/deformation.py:432). The right extension is shared rest geometry with explicit component boundaries, hinges, and per-photo camera/temple poses. Keep the cage for small residual corrections. Preserve provider texture and local detail where useful; do not force every frame into a crude generic template.

3. **Lens material and photographed lighting are confused.** A white reflection and white transmission can give the same pixel. A reflected softbox can resemble a tint gradient. The current constant/smooth environment models cannot explain all studio bands, so the fit can compensate by changing the material into a mirror. Roughness is also a fixed prior rather than recovered from these observations. See [photo_lens_fit.py:434](C:/Users/Shay/PycharmProjects/lenses/automation/reconstruction/photo_lens_fit.py:434) and [joint_photo_lens_fit.py:392](C:/Users/Shay/PycharmProjects/lenses/automation/reconstruction/joint_photo_lens_fit.py:392). Improve the reflection model and use optional catalog facts when available, but do not assume a more flexible environment resolves ambiguity. Without identifying evidence, the output needs an explicit prior or an uncertain-result status. The Oakley response also warrants testing richer angle-dependent color models against independent examples before extending the renderer.

4. **The quality score covers selected evidence, not the whole product.** Poorly registered views, rear geometry seen through lenses, unsupported stacks and other ambiguous samples are excluded. The remaining pixels can pass while a lens has little useful view coverage. In addition, held-out pixels choose optimizer/mask representatives and material families; they are a model-selection set, not an untouched final test. See [joint_photo_lens_stage.py:136](C:/Users/Shay/PycharmProjects/lenses/automation/reconstruction/joint_photo_lens_stage.py:136), [appearance_selection.py:44](C:/Users/Shay/PycharmProjects/lenses/automation/reconstruction/appearance_selection.py:44), and [joint_photo_lens_fit.py:374](C:/Users/Shay/PycharmProjects/lenses/automation/reconstruction/joint_photo_lens_fit.py:374). Exclusions are recorded honestly. They must also affect the eventual whole-product verdict. Historical acceptance has already produced a passing gray Ray-Ban where tortoiseshell was expected.

5. **More input views can exceed the current solver's limits.** With three distinct region alternatives, front/back each observing two lenses plus left/right each observing one gives six region rows: 729 combinations. The default cap is 243; the corresponding five-family, two-lighting, three-start exploration would require about 21,870 starts against a 2,430 default. Duplicate alternatives and absent observations can reduce this, so not every four-photo input fails. Nevertheless, general four-plus-view fitting is unfinished. See [joint_photo_lens_fit.py:57](C:/Users/Shay/PycharmProjects/lenses/automation/reconstruction/joint_photo_lens_fit.py:57) and [photo_lens_fit.py:380](C:/Users/Shay/PycharmProjects/lenses/automation/reconstruction/photo_lens_fit.py:380). The [scalable fitter proposal](C:/Users/Shay/PycharmProjects/lenses/automation/plan/SCALABLE_PHOTO_FIT.md:3) is not integrated. For more than three views the grouping stage also restricts aperture interpretations to all-full or all-crop, omitting mixed choices. Saved fresh requests use front/angled for local refinement and fitting, with back/left/right as provider-only inputs; this does not demonstrate local five-view reconstruction.

Meshy's current API itself takes at most four images, with the first as the primary view. Selecting a diverse subset for generation is reasonable; additional photos should still inform reconstruction and evaluation. [Official Meshy API documentation](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/api/multi-image-to-3d).

**The implementation sequence I recommend.**

1. **Define and freeze the visual evaluation first.** Retain the five development products, add a reserved set of distinct products spanning clear, tinted, gradient, mirrored, shield, rimless, thin and thick frames, and judge final exported assets in actual AR. Use existing photos. Separate photographs used for generation, fitting, model selection and final evaluation; a view seen by Meshy is not an independent geometry holdout. Where available photos cannot support a holdout, label the evaluation as such. Record missing components, contour/proportion errors, lens/frame assignment, pattern preservation, optical appearance, coverage and compute cost. Include intentional bad outputs to prove the checks catch wrong tint, missing arms, filled apertures and lost frame patterns.

2. **Fix the bounded automation defects and make many-view inference practical.** Correct generation-to-split continuation, shared estimated budget accounting and exterior duplicate handling. Integrate factorized or bounded alternating mask/material inference rather than increasing the exhaustive-search cap. Preserve uncertainty and compare the scalable search with exhaustive cases small enough to verify. Ensure additional useful photographs help rather than exhaust the budget. Handle transparent-background catalog assets explicitly.

3. **Move image understanding ahead of geometry refinement.** Reuse the independent aperture and component projection work. Estimate cameras against appropriate frame, lens-boundary and temple evidence; permit template-independent component hypotheses. Jointly refine shared product geometry and per-photo pose/articulation. Add smooth lens/edge and attachment constraints where justified by visible structure. Retain source-preserving closed/multipart groups; do not restart the failed front-envelope conversion as a universal solution.

4. **Fit appearance with AR behavior as the target.** Keep absorption, spatial gradients and angular reflection separate. Use constrained per-photo lighting shared by both lenses, optional known product facts, and bounded competing material explanations. Test candidates in several fixed environments and angles in the real consumer. Such renders can reveal unstable alternatives but cannot establish the true material without reference evidence. Use independent references and visual judgments to choose the approximation policy. Frame textures also need evaluation: transferred provider textures can contain baked studio highlights and shadows.

5. **Package for production after appearance is convincing.** Build an automatic result report/preview and a clear supported/uncertain/failed outcome. Normalize the export coordinates expected by AR and preserve relative proportions; leave wearer-width fitting to the existing runtime. Optimize geometry/textures without damaging contours or materials and test on mobile. The inspected candidates are approximately 21.31/38.06/20.53 MiB with 252,920/641,558/197,442 triangles for Miu/Oakley/VB, so mobile asset cost deserves measurement. Saved runtime qualification still lists a strict light-depth discrepancy and lacks sustained mobile evidence; these are separate from photographic fidelity.

I would make the next deliverable a single reproducible batch run on reserved products, with side-by-side photos and actual AR renders, explicit failure categories and no product-specific edits during evaluation. That would tell us whether each change improves the automation. Existing arbitrary photographs cannot uniquely expose every hidden shape or material property, so the useful product promise is a strong automatic reconstruction with a measured supported scope and honest uncertainty, rather than an unconditional claim of perfection.
