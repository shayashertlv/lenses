## 1. WHAT THIS IS

A browser eyewear try-on. Three programs in sequence:

1. **Enrollment** — a short guided face scan (7 beats: centre, two turns, two nods, two
   leans). One joint bundle adjustment solves head poses, a 20-mode anthropometric shape
   basis, a free-form per-vertex nose displacement field, and the camera's focal length,
   producing a personal metric `FaceModel`. Absolute scale comes from the iris.
2. **Tracking** — per-frame PnP of six pose parameters against that solved model, with a
   One Euro filter, a constant-velocity motion prior fused into the normal equations, a
   per-vertex rigidity map, visibility culling, and four hard refusal gates.
3. **Fit** — a rigid-body contact-physics solve places the glasses on the nose ONCE per
   (face, frame) pair and caches the transform. Rendering is three.js; the face is also
   rasterised as a depth occluder so temples disappear behind the head.

Everything runs in the browser. No camera frame or face model leaves the device.

**A fit ADVISOR was deleted on 2026-09-04** — `src/fit/score.ts`, all grades, millimetre
verdicts, the fit score and the catalogue ranking, ~1,600 lines. It is not coming back and
it is not a gap. If you find any surviving reference to it that implies it is live, that
is a defect and I want it reported. `fit/` now exists only to place a frame so it can be
DRAWN. The seat is a rendering input, not a claim about the wearer.

## 2. THE PRIME DIRECTIVE

**This codebase's comments are long, confident, self-critical, and frequently wrong.**

That is not a slur — the project documents its own retractions, which is why so many stale
claims are visible. But it means the single highest-value thing you can do is check
prose against code, everywhere, and it means you must never take a comment as evidence.

Concretely, these have all been found in this tree and fixed, and more remain:

- A header enumerating a module's callers as "both of them, and that is the whole list"
  when there are five.

When code and comment disagree, **the code is what ships and the comment is the bug**, but
report both: which is wrong depends on which one the rest of the tree believes.

### src/core/ — linalg.ts, camera.ts, robust.ts, raster.ts (the arithmetic layer: SO(3)/SE(3), dense LDL^T, pinhole+k1 camera and its four analytic jacob…

**`src/core/linalg.ts`** — Float64, out-parameter Vec3/Mat3/Pose arithmetic; Rodrigues exp/log on SO(3) with tiny-angle and near-pi branches; the pose-increment convention (R<-exp(w)R, t<-t+u); in-place LDL^T factorise/solve/invert for small dense systems; YXZ euler and quaternion conversions; weighted median, MAD, percentile, clamp, smoothstep. Units are mm throughout core/.
  -> expSO3 (137), logSO3 (168), poseOplus (296), orthonormalize (213), ldlt (406), ldltSolve (422), eulerYXZ (359), weightedMedian (470)
**`src/core/camera.ts`** — Pinhole camera with one focal length, central principal point and a single radial k1; forward projection, fixed-point undistorting ray lift, and the four hand-written analytic jacobians (d(uv)/dXcam, /dpose-increment, /dXmodel, /dintrinsics) the bundle and PnP consume. Also owns the face-space<->CV-camera pi-about-X flip that turns a model-to-camera pose into wearer yaw/pitch/roll, and the rule for carrying a solved camera to a different source resolution.
  -> project (165), dProjDPoint (257), dProjDPose (333), dProjDModelPoint (364), dProjDIntrinsics (395), headEuler (468), poseRotationFromHeadEuler (503), scaleIntrinsics (144)
**`src/core/robust.ts`** — Robust M-estimator losses expressed as [rho(s), rho'(s)] over the SQUARED whitened residual, so rho' is directly the IRLS weight on both hessian and gradient. Huber (the only family actually used in src/), Cauchy, and Barron's one-parameter family whose alpha the tracker schedules continuously per correspondence from +1 to -2; plus a 1.4826*MAD scale estimator.
  -> huber (57), barron (150), barronDrho (171), barronRho (177), robustScale (213)
**`src/core/raster.ts`** — A scanline software rasteriser producing a camera-space depth buffer (Float32, mm) and a triangle-id buffer from a posed mesh; then per-vertex visibility by comparing each vertex's own depth against the buffer with a facing-dependent tolerance, an occluding-contour extractor, and a normals-to-camera transform. It is the tree's single answer to 'what is in front of what' for the non-GPU consumers.
  -> rasterize (307), vertexVisibility (391), createDepthBuffer (284), extractSilhouette (438), normalsToCamera (457)

*Where the difficulty is:*
- **logSO3's near-pi branch: axis recovered from the symmetric part, sign from a vanishing antisymmetric part** (`src/core/linalg.ts:180-203`) — Three sub-cases (k=0/1/2) plus an s<=EPS fallthrough, and at exactly pi the sign is genuinely ambiguous so sgn=0 silently picks +theta. A wrong-sign axis returns the right magnitude and the wrong direction; the only consumer that reaches it is a large keyframe-relative rotation, where the failure reads as a dropped keyframe pair, not a NaN. tests/core.test.ts:101 samples the branch but not per-k.
- **The pose-increment convention is fixed in one function and assumed by every jacobian in the tree** (`src/core/linalg.ts:296 with src/core/camera.ts:333`) — dProjDPose requires Prot = R*X_model WITHOUT the translation. All three call sites build `rot` by hand (src/enroll/bundle.ts:738, :886, src/track/pnp.ts:531). Passing `cam` instead is exactly correct at t=0 and degrades with |t| — converges slowly to a slightly wrong answer, indistinguishable from noise. Only the central-difference test (tests/core.test.ts:352) can see it.
- **ldlt leaves the upper triangle of A untouched; solveSymmetric returns with A in a hybrid state** (`src/core/linalg.ts:406-441`) — After solveSymmetric, A is neither the input nor a full factorisation — lower triangle is L/D, upper is the original data. invertSymmetric defends itself with a copy (444-445); solveSymmetric does not, and its docstring calls this 'leaving A factorised'. Any caller reusing A (recomputing a cost, extracting a covariance) is silently wrong. The positive-definite floor 1e-14 is also absolute, not norm-relative, so the same system in different units passes or fails.
- **Module-scope scratch buffers in the two composed jacobians** (`src/core/camera.ts:316-320, used at 336, 344, 367`) — dPtPose/dPtModel/skewScratch are shared mutable state whose correctness rests on: both functions being leaves, neither being reentrant, each realm getting its own module instance, and skewScratch's three diagonal zeros never being written after construction. dProjDPose runs ~2.2M times per enrollment and ~3,800 times per live frame, so this is load-bearing perf, not tidiness. Two tests pin it (tests/core.test.ts:440, :495) but the invariant binds every future edit.
- **applyIntrinsicsDelta clamps inside an LM step, and the clamp is absent from the jacobian** (`src/core/camera.ts:426-428`) — f is floored at width*0.2 and k1 clamped to +/-0.6 after the step is computed. dProjDIntrinsics knows nothing about either, so on a clamped iteration LM's predicted-vs-actual cost reduction disagree and the damping heuristic misreads a good model as a bad one. Invisible unless the solve is watched at the boundary.
- **Depth is interpolated affinely in screen space, not perspective-correct, and visibility is judged at a 1.5 mm tolerance** (`src/core/raster.ts:367 with :394,:415`) — z = w1*az + w2*bz + w0*cz is linear in projected coordinates. The error is largest on exactly the grazing triangles where the slope term already widens the tolerance to 7.5 mm, and on the same triangles the both-windings comment (345-348) says must not be dropped. Nothing in tests/ differences a rasterised depth against an analytic one on an oblique triangle.
- **vertexVisibility returns a facing COSINE, not a visibility, and it is the shared instrument for both truth and estimate** (`src/core/raster.ts:391-423, callers src/detect/uncertainty.ts:176 and src/testkit/synthet…`) — With normals supplied, out[i] = max(0,-nz) — a back-facing but depth-unoccluded vertex returns 0, identical to a truly occluded one, and an edge-on visible vertex also returns 0. Both callers pass normals and take the default tolerances, so synthetic ground-truth observability and the runtime uncertainty down-weighting are the SAME function with the SAME constants: the estimator cannot be graded against a truth it shares. testkit/synthetic.ts:1105-1123 comments around this without breaking the coupling.
- **DepthBuffer.scale is frozen at construction from one Intrinsics; rasterize recomputes its scale from a different argument** (`src/core/raster.ts:290 vs :312, consumed at :441`) — createDepthBuffer sets scale from the CONSTRUCTION intrinsics; rasterize computes s from the ARGUMENT intrinsics; extractSilhouette divides by buffer.scale. Disagreeing intrinsics silently rescale every returned silhouette coordinate. Both cache guards defend it by comparing intrinsics with object IDENTITY (src/detect/uncertainty.ts:163-169, src/app/main.ts:1192-1197), so the invariant depends on a caller's allocation pattern rather than on values.
- **Barron's family near alpha = 0** (`src/core/robust.ts:177-188 with the schedule at src/track/tracker.ts:1160`) — alpha sweeps continuously from BARRON_ALPHA_HIGH=1 to BARRON_ALPHA_LOW=-2 per correspondence, so it lands arbitrarily close to but essentially never exactly at 0 — the `alpha === 0` exact-equality branch is dead in practice and the general expm1 branch carries the crossing. That branch multiplies (2*c2*b)/alpha by expm1(...); the factors cancel analytically, but for |alpha| below ~1e-300 the first overflows before the second underflows. The header claims accuracy only to 1e-12.
- **Two different median conventions feeding two different sigma estimates** (`src/core/linalg.ts:470-501 vs src/core/robust.ts:213-219`) — weightedMedian averages the two central values on even n; robustScale takes the upper one and reimplements MAD inline rather than calling the tested mad() its own docstring points at (robust.ts:211). They disagree on any even-length sample, and robustScale's entire reason to exist is a 1.4826 consistency constant that assumes a specific median.
- **headEuler's left-multiply by the face flip is the difference between a working guided scan and one that can never advance** (`src/core/camera.ts:466-494`) — FLIP*R recovers head rotation; R*FLIP returns the conjugate, which negates yaw and roll while leaving pitch correct. The header records that the synthetic generator once made the mirrored error, so 56 tests passed over a system that inverted yaw on every real face. Any test that builds a pose with poseRotationFromHeadEuler (503) and reads it back with headEuler is self-consistent by construction and cannot detect a re-introduced conjugation — only an image-space observable can.
- **eulerYXZ's gimbal branch threshold and the yaw sign flip past |pitch| > 90 degrees** (`src/core/linalg.ts:359-367`) — The branch fires only at |R[5]| >= 0.9999999 (~0.026 deg of vertical), but the non-branch yaw = atan2(R[2], R[8]) is off by pi for any cos(pitch) < 0. There is a whole band of steep pitch where the formula is silently wrong and the guard has not fired.

### src/core/ — mesh, meshdist, head, facemodel

**`src/core/mesh.ts`** — Parses the 468-vertex MediaPipe canonical .obj (cm->mm at parse), builds vertex adjacency and area-weighted normals, grows six feathered graph-distance regions (nose/padStrip/bridge/eyes/temples/cheeks), builds per-oval-landmark 'silhouette strips' of candidate contour vertices for the tracker's landmark marching, builds the per-vertex pose rigidity map (lid rings zeroed, eye surround half-weighted), and computes the nine-number FaceMeasurements anthropom…
  -> parseFaceObj, standardRegions/growRegion, silhouetteStrips, trackingRigidity, measure, computeVertexNormals
**`src/core/meshdist.ts`** — Exact signed closest-point-on-mesh query accelerated by a uniform 6 mm grid over triangle AABBs; returns the surface point, an interpolated (not face) normal, a signed distance (positive = outside skin) and the unsigned magnitude. Sole consumer is the contact solver, which differentiates its pose energy through this distance.
  -> buildMeshDistance (and its inner query/testTriangle), closestPointOnTriangle, emptyClosestPoint
**`src/core/head.ts`** — Closes the open face mesh into a head occluder: finds the 36-vertex boundary loop from single-owner edges, lofts it backwards through 12 superellipse rings to an occipital pole sharing the rim's own vertices, and welds on two open parabolic pinna dishes placed from the temple arms' ear-rest points.
  -> buildHeadWithEars, buildHeadShell, boundaryLoop, reloftSkull, skullHalfWidthAt, buildPinna/pinnaPlacement
**`src/core/facemodel.ts`** — Defines the immutable FaceModel (positions, per-vertex sigma, shape coeffs, landmark bias, scale estimate, per-region quality, scan record) plus its FNV-1a geometry hash, JSON round-trip with a hard version-3 refusal, the detector-convention surface (positions + landmarkBiasMm), and noseConfidence — a three-term product (parallax x observed x variance-factor agreement) with a reason string naming the binding term.
  -> createFaceModel, serializeFaceModel/deserializeFaceModel, landmarkSurface, hashGeometry, noseConfidence

*Where the difficulty is:*
- **silhouetteStrips candidate selection may exclude the landmark's own vertex** (`src/core/mesh.ts:422-436`) — Candidates are every vertex within heightMm=6 of the landmark's y, same x-sign, sorted by |x| desc, then sliced to 20. Nothing guarantees the landmark itself survives the slice, so the identity correspondence can be unavailable to the tracker's march. A wrong strip does not fail — the tracker still converges, just onto a biased vertex. The docstring itself admits 10 of the 34 strips remap by 11.7-20.0 mm at exact frontal pose and that this is KEPT (mesh.ts:372-381).
- **Sign(x) midline test silently drops exactly-midline vertices from every strip** (`src/core/mesh.ts:425`) — Math.sign(0)===0 never equals Math.sign(x0)===±1, so any vertex at x=0.0 is in no strip's candidate set on either side. Interacts with the separate midlineMm=5 landmark skip at :421. Both are template-specific cuts (the docstring says 'exactly two landmarks at x=0.0'); a template with slight asymmetry changes both behaviours with no test signal.
- **The region feather is load-bearing everywhere except in the quality summary, which re-binarises it** (`src/core/mesh.ts:250-258 vs src/enroll/enroll.ts:602`) — growRegion deliberately produces smoothstep weights (crease avoidance), but summarise() drops every member with weight < 0.5 before averaging observations/parallax/sigma. So RegionQuality describes the region's core only, while the displacement field and contact solver see the feathered whole. Changing a region radius/feather moves the confidence numbers through a channel nobody documents.
- **The expanding-shell early exit in the grid query** (`src/core/meshdist.ts:179-201`) — Termination is `found && (ring-1)*cellSize > sqrt(best)`, best being squared distance. It is correct only because triangles are inserted into every cell their AABB touches, so a triangle's closest point lies in one of its own cells. Any future change to the insertion (e.g. exact tri-box overlap instead of AABB) breaks the bound and the query silently returns a non-nearest triangle — and the SIGN then comes from that wrong triangle's normal, which is the contact physics.
- **The sign is a dot product against an interpolated normal, and it is arbitrary at zero distance** (`src/core/meshdist.ts:157`) — `sign = (d · n) >= 0 ? 1 : -1` where d is the vector from the closest point. At |d| -> 0 (a pad exactly on skin, the operating point of the solve) the dot product is noise, so `distance` can flip ±magnitude between adjacent solver iterations. The contact energy is differentiated through this. Nothing in the return says the sign is untrustworthy except `magnitude`, which callers must check themselves.
- **Degenerate-triangle path in closestPointOnTriangle** (`src/core/meshdist.ts:260-265`) — The final barycentric branch divides by (va+vb+vc); for a zero-area triangle that is 0, giving denom=Infinity and NaN barycentrics -> NaN normal -> NaN distance propagating into the contact solve. No guard. Reaching it requires a degenerate triangle in the wearer's deformed positions, not the template, so a test on the template cannot see it.
- **boundaryLoop's ring DIRECTION is the occluder's correctness, and it comes from one directed-edge lookup** (`src/core/head.ts:170-189`) — nextOf maps b->a for an edge wound a->b, i.e. walks AGAINST the winding, which is what makes the loft wind outward. An inward shell writes no depth (back-face culled) and the temple arms X-ray through the skull with every test green — the file documents that exact failure at head.ts:148-169. nextOf is a Map keyed by vertex: a non-manifold or multi-loop boundary silently overwrites entries and the walk just stops early (`ring.length < 8` is the only guard, head.ts:203).
- **The loft's vertex-index arithmetic is written twice, in two functions, and must agree** (`src/core/head.ts:213-238 and src/core/head.ts:277-298`) — buildHeadShell allocates (faceVertexCount + (SKULL_RINGS-1)*R + 1) vertices and indexes them via its local `at(k,i)`; reloftSkull recomputes the same addresses inline (`faceVertexCount + (k-1)*R + i`, pole at `faceVertexCount + (SKULL_RINGS-1)*R`). An off-by-one in one and not the other writes the pole over the last ring, or writes past the buffer, and the symptom is a geometry artefact behind the head that the software rasteriser's double-winding tolerance may hide.
- **skullHalfWidthAt is a third, independent restatement of the loft's width law** (`src/core/head.ts:419-427`) — It re-derives `(1 - s^FULLNESS)^(1/FULLNESS)` from ringCentre[2] and the ring's max |x|, ignoring `depthScale` (which reloftSkull applies to the real geometry) and using the ring's widest point rather than the width at the ear's own y. The result decides where the pinna dish seats; too small and the ear sinks into the skull, refilling the crevice the ear exists to keep open.
- **One ear's z decides both ears' skull half-width** (`src/core/head.ts:386`) — `skullHalfWidthAt(shell, earRests[0][2])` is computed once and passed to both pinnaPlacement calls. Correct only while the two rest points are z-symmetric. Any asymmetric fit (a real wearer's solved rests) seats one ear at the wrong depth's width, and the artefact is an arm crossing a pinna on one side only.
- **The skin / landmark-convention split is currently invisible because the bias is zero** (`src/core/facemodel.ts:392-411`) — `positions` is skin (enroll subtracts bias); everything comparing against the detector must use landmarkSurface(). detector-bias.ts ships an all-zero offset, so the two surfaces are identical today and every call site agrees by accident. The first non-zero calibration makes every missed call site wrong at ~3.4 mm of pose error per mm of bias (detector-bias.ts:38-42), with reprojection moving only 0.71->0.94 px, i.e. no gate fires.
- **noseConfidence's three terms are multiplicative and two of their calibrations are stale by construction** (`src/core/facemodel.ts:609-648`) — TYPICAL_VARIANCE_FACTOR=1.9 is self-documented as measured at keyframes=48 and 'not re-measured at 24' (facemodel.ts:534-537), and NOSE_OBSERVED_FRACTION=0.40 was set just under a synthetic band that the file also says a real neck cannot reach (facemodel.ts:552-556). A product of three [0,1] terms compounds both. The `worst` scan uses strict `<` starting from terms[0], so on the all-zero degraded path (enroll.ts:636) the reason is always 'not enough head turn' regardless of which term actually failed.

### src/core/shape/ — the face model basis and displacement field

**`src/core/shape/anthropometric.ts`** — Constructs the 20-mode identity basis from the template mesh alone: 20 hand-written smooth displacement fields over the 468 vertices, each scaled so its PEAK per-vertex displacement equals one population SD of a named craniofacial trait (CVs transferred as ratios, applied to spans measured on the template), then Gram-Schmidt'd and pruned. Also provides basisExplains, the least-squares residual of a target shape against a basis.
  -> buildAnthropometricBasis (121), the generators table (176-265), basisExplains (359)
**`src/core/shape/basis.ts`** — Defines the ShapeBasis slot interface (mean, modes, sigma, labels) and the three operations on it: linear evaluation, per-vertex/per-mode Jacobian, and modified Gram-Schmidt of generator fields with a rescale, plus removal of fully-dependent modes.
  -> ShapeBasis (40), evaluateBasis (73), orthonormalizeModes (115), pruneBasis (155)
**`src/core/shape/displacement.ts`** — The free-form nose patch: one scalar per nose-region vertex, displacing along a frozen surface normal scaled by the region feather weight. Builds the slot map and the field-restricted neighbour lists, applies/differentiates the field, and accumulates the uniform-Laplacian + magnitude priors as a full rank-one outer product into the normal equations.
  -> createDisplacementField (67), applyDisplacement (118), displacementJacobian (133), accumulateDisplacementPriors (203), DISPLACEMENT_PRIORS (167)

*Where the difficulty is:*
- **orthonormalizeModes' normalise-then-rescale is algebraically the identity: v/norm * norm = v, so the returned modes are just the Gram-Schmidt RESIDUALS — orthogonal but not orthon…** (`src/core/shape/basis.ts:139-150`) — The function's name, its docstring and its inline comment all describe a rescale that does nothing. A reviewer will burn time deciding whether the output is unit-norm (it is not) and whether a normalisation was lost.
- **basisExplains computes every coefficient from the ORIGINAL delta by independent projection, then subtracts all modes — a least-squares fit only because the modes happen to be mutu…** (`src/core/shape/anthropometric.ts:365-377`) — The orthogonality invariant is produced in basis.ts and consumed here, enforced only by tests/pipeline.test.ts:185-198. Drop a non-orthogonal basis into the slot (the whole point of the interface) and this silently returns a non-LS answer with no error; the recovery test at pipeline.test.ts:234-249 builds its target FROM the basis so it cannot see it either.
- **A mode annihilated by Gram-Schmidt is dropped by pruneBasis while `name: 'anthropometric-20'` stays a literal.** (`src/core/shape/basis.ts:131-137 -> anthropometric.ts:342-350`) — This is the exact failure the file's own comments say already happened once (browRidge). The throw at anthropometric.ts:288 catches only an identically-zero GENERATOR, not a GS-annihilated one. Only tests/core.test.ts:921 (dim === 20) can catch the GS path; tests/pipeline.test.ts:169 accepts >= 15 and would stay green.
- **Peak normalisation (s = magnitude/peak) is applied BEFORE Gram-Schmidt, so the shipped scale of every mode after the first depends on the ORDER of the generators array.** (`src/core/shape/anthropometric.ts:334 and the ordering at 176-265`) — The array order is a silent numerical contract with no test. Reordering two entries changes every downstream coefficient's meaning, the shapePrior's effective strength per trait, and testkit/synthetic.ts's drawn population — all without failing anything.
- **ramp() clamps, so an inverted (lo > hi) pair returns a constant with no error; that is precisely how browRidge shipped as an identically-zero field.** (`src/core/shape/anthropometric.ts:150-153, used at 241-242, 248, 251, 254, 264`) — Every ramp bound is an arithmetic expression over template landmark spans (browSpan*0.18, browSpan*0.55, chinY/eyeY/foreheadY). A wrong span silently produces a constant field, not a crash, and a constant field is only visible as a peak or a placement test.
- **The region feather weight multiplies the Jacobian and applyDisplacement but NOT the priors: the Laplacian and magnitude terms penalise raw `values`, while the surface actually mov…** (`src/core/shape/displacement.ts:122 and 137 vs 222-223 and 250`) — At the feathered rim (weight -> 0) the prior charges for a displacement the surface never makes, so the rim is regularised in the wrong currency; and displacementStats:267 reports the WEIGHTED value, so the field's own RMS readout and the objective it was solved under measure different quantities.
- **The Laplacian's H contribution is written as the complete m x m outer product, lower triangle only, with `+=` for repeated parameter indices.** (`src/core/shape/displacement.ts:235-243`) — PSD-ness (and therefore whether ldlt succeeds and the whole nose stage runs at all — see the header at 175-202) depends on writing every neighbour-vs-neighbour pair. The duplicate-index case is only correct because both (p,q) and (q,p) satisfy i >= j when i === j; that is load-bearing and unstated.
- **The displacement objective is written twice, in two files, and the line search compares one against the other.** (`src/core/shape/displacement.ts:214-255 vs src/enroll/bundle.ts:1304-1315`) — accumulateDisplacementPriors skips a slot whose degree+1 exceeds the 32-entry scratch (line 217) and charges it NO smoothness cost; costField charges smoothness for any deg > 0. On a degree-32 slot the gradient/Hessian and the line-search cost describe different objectives, and the line search would reject good steps with no diagnostic.
- **The frozen-normal approximation: refreshNormals recomputes area-weighted normals over the WHOLE mesh and copies the nose slots; the Jacobian omits d(normal)/d(value).** (`src/core/shape/displacement.ts:105-115, 133-140`) — The header (38-45) is candid that the only test (tests/core.test.ts:545-575) differences displacementJacobian against applyDisplacement, which rides the same frozen field.normals — both sides make the identical approximation, so the neglected term is unmeasured and structurally unmeasurable by that test.
- **The `map` parameter's -1 ("held fixed") path is never exercised: the sole caller builds an identity map.** (`src/core/shape/displacement.ts:206, 228, 233, 248 vs src/enroll/bundle.ts:1139-1140`) — Roughly a third of the branching in the prior accumulator has never run. A bug there (wrong index, skipped gradient term) is invisible today and would surface only if a future partial-solve used it.
- **Slot topology and feather weights are frozen from the TEMPLATE geometry, while the solve moves the positions those weights and the Laplacian were derived from.** (`src/core/shape/displacement.ts:71-101 (region.weight and adjacency read once) with the re…`) — The Laplacian is uniform (1/deg), not cotangent, so it is already blind to edge lengths; combined with frozen weights, the prior's spatial scale is a property of the template, not of the wearer, and no readout says so.
- **`tipness` clamps to 1 for every vertex at or below the subnasale, so the four modes built on it load the alae, not the nose tip.** (`src/core/shape/anthropometric.ts:161, used at 198, 211, 214, 260`) — The comment says "1 at the nose tip" and it is 1 at the subnasale and below. noseProtrusion, noseSidewallFlare, noseTipDroop and noseDeviation all inherit that placement — and this region is exactly where a nose pad bears, so a misplaced peak here is a seat error nothing in the basis tests would catch.

### src/enroll/ — bundle adjustment (bundle.ts) and the scan entry point (enroll.ts)

**`src/enroll/bundle.ts`** — Joint solve for the wearer: alternates stage A (per-frame 6-dof poses + shape coefficients + intrinsics, poses Schur-eliminated into a ~G×G reduced system, LM with lambda damping) against stage B (a per-nose-vertex scalar displacement field along frozen normals, one damped Gauss-Newton step with a 3-length backtracking line search), for `rounds` rounds with the Huber threshold ramped huberStart→huberEnd. Residuals are landmark reprojection whitened by sqr…
  -> runBundle (430), solveGlobal (570), accumulateGlobal (684), solveField (1132), accumulateSilhouette (823), contourVertices (933), landmarkRigidity (388), reprojectionStats (1431), BUNDLE_DEFAULTS (164)
**`src/enroll/enroll.ts`** — Six-step scan pipeline: sequential PnP pose init (frame N seeded from N-1, depth gate 50..5000, <30 correspondences or <8 surviving frames ⇒ degraded template return), keyframe selection + coverage assessment (which also decides whether focal length may be solved), runBundle, scale (iris via solveScale then an optional wearer-PD correction taken off the solved 3-D eye-corner span, both applied through applyScale to positions/pose translations/field values…
  -> enroll (89), perVertexUncertainty (487), interpupillarySpan (459), summarise (596), degraded (628)

*Where the difficulty is:*
- **Schur elimination silently skips a frame whose 6×6 Hpp will not factorise** (`src/enroll/bundle.ts:604`) — `if (!ldlt(work,6)) continue;` leaves ok[f]=0, so that frame's pose block is never folded out of `reduced` and never back-substituted — but its contribution to Hgg/gg was already accumulated at 763-775. The reduced system then treats that frame's shape/intrinsics information as if its pose were fixed and known. dp stays 0. Nothing counts or reports it; no test covers a rank-deficient pose block.
- **Four independent implementations of the same projection residual must stay numerically identical** (`src/enroll/bundle.ts:684, 823, 1132, 1281, 1299, 1334, 1363, 986, 1431`) — accumulateGlobal/accumulateSilhouette build cost while building the Hessian; costGlobal/costLandmarks/costSilhouetteOnly rebuild it for the LM accept test; solveField keeps a third running total; countSilhouette and reprojectionStats re-run the loop again. Accept tests straddle these implementations (668, 1261). A term, gate or weight added to one and not the others silently changes which steps are accepted — the file records that exact failure with the silhouette (1114-1130).
- **solveField's line search compares two different cost estimators** (`src/enroll/bundle.ts:1255-1263`) — `bestCost` starts at the accumulation's running total (data + priors summed during Hessian assembly) but each trial is scored by `costField`. They agree only because both were hand-written to cover the same terms (claimed 4.4e-15 relative at 1270-1275). If they diverge, steps are accepted or rejected on a constant offset, and the field silently freezes (bestAlpha 0 is not counted anywhere).
- **The half-gradient / half-Hessian convention (dropped factor of 2) must hold across every term** (`src/enroll/bundle.ts:756-775, 791-797, 1177-1178, 1220-1221`) — Data terms accumulate g += drho·Jᵀr and H += drho·JᵀJ (½ of the true derivatives of rho=r²); the shape prior at 791-797 matches with g += w·rr, H += w². A wrong factor on one term does not break convergence — it re-weights that term by 2×, which reads as mis-tuning, not a bug.
- **Huber's threshold is in different units for landmarks and for the silhouette** (`src/enroll/bundle.ts:731 vs 879; claim contradicted at src/core/robust.ts:52`) — Landmark residuals are whitened to sigma units (sqrt(rigidity)/sigmaPx); silhouette residuals are raw pixels × silhouetteWeight. huber(4.0) therefore means 4 sigma for one and ~4 px for the other. BundleOptions (121-129) admits a ~3× currency gap; robust.ts:52 says the threshold means the same thing for both. Every silhouetteWeight sweep is entangled with the robustifier's cut point.
- **Gauge/units invariant spanning enroll → scale → facemodel** (`src/enroll/enroll.ts:194, 212; src/enroll/scale.ts:431-453`) — applyScale multiplies positions, pose translations and field.values — but NOT shapeCoeffs, NOT intrinsics, and updateGeometry is never called again afterwards (it would undo the scaling by rebuilding positions from the unscaled basis). Everything after 194 (the 1/(8·8) information floor, detector-bias subtraction, displacementStats) assumes mm. The PD branch applies applyScale a second time, so the factor is a product.
- **varianceFactor's redundancy counts a gated residual subset against the full parameter count** (`src/enroll/bundle.ts:532-539, 1443`) — reprojectionStats counts only vertices with rigidity ≥ 0.5 (plus valid sigma, non-NaN landmark, in front of camera), yet redundancy = 2·count − (6·frames + basis.dim + intrinsicsDof) — parameters constrained by residuals the count excludes. varianceFactor scales every reported per-vertex sigma (enroll.ts:373-374) and feeds noseConfidence (facemodel.ts:627), so a bias here is invisible and wearer-facing.
- **Per-vertex parallax is the length of a weighted resultant of unit view directions** (`src/enroll/enroll.ts:539-548, 573-578`) — viewSum accumulates w·unit(−Rᵀ·cam); parallax = 2·asin(sqrt((1−R)/2)) with R = |mean|. A transpose sign error, or normalising after weighting instead of before, is invisible on symmetric captures and shows only as a mild constant. Its predecessor metric was pinned at 1.0 on every device without anyone noticing.
- **The silhouette grid's correctness rests on cell size == match radius, exactly** (`src/enroll/bundle.ts:1051, 1062, 1086-1098`) — nearestSilhouette visits only 3×3 cells; a cell smaller than SILHOUETTE_MATCH_PX misses in-radius points. A null is both how a point is refused and how silhouetteResiduals decides the term ran, so a shrunken cell shrinks the term and its own reporting together. Pinned only by a brute-force comparison in tests/pipeline.test.ts.
- **Module-level mutable failure counter makes runBundle non-reentrant** (`src/enroll/bundle.ts:1105, 434, 526`) — `fieldFactorisationFailures` is a file-scoped let zeroed at the top of runBundle. Two overlapping bundles in one JS context (worker plus main, or a harness loop) cross-contaminate `converged` and `fieldFailures`.
- **createBundleState and runBundle refresh the field's normals against different surfaces** (`src/enroll/bundle.ts:420 (basis+field) vs 472 (basisOnly)`) — Harmless only because the field is zero at construction. A caller constructing state with a non-zero field starts round 0 with normals from a different surface than every later round.
- **enroll's degraded early return happens before coverage is computed and hand-builds both a CoverageVerdict and a BundleReport** (`src/enroll/enroll.ts:117-120, 628-673`) — Those literals must stay structurally in sync with two interfaces in other files; any added field silently defaults (varianceFactor is already omitted and defaulted to 1 at facemodel.ts:352), so the failure path can report something it never measured.

### src/enroll/ — protocol, keyframes, scale, detector-bias, telemetry

**`src/enroll/protocol.ts`** — The guided scan as a 7-beat state machine: converts a solved Pose into a yaw/pitch/roll/distance sample, learns the wearer's neutral from the opening `centre` beat, and decides per frame whether the current beat is satisfied (threshold beats) or has plateaued (reach beats), giving up after a frame budget. Emits per-frame UI state and a small immutable ScanRecord that outlives the protocol on the FaceModel.
  -> advanceProtocol (381), sampleFromPose (618), BEATS (162), createProtocol (332), scanRecord (320), summarise (644)
**`src/enroll/keyframes.ts`** — Picks the ~24 frames the bundle solves against — frontal anchors, then a guaranteed frame at each of six per-axis extremes, then farthest-point sampling in a weighted (yaw, pitch, roll, logZ) pose space — and reports the resulting yaw/pitch/distance spans. `assessCoverage` turns those spans into `sufficient` (sets model.degraded), `canSolveIntrinsics` (gates the focal-length solve) and `hasProfile`, plus one line of wearer advice.
  -> selectKeyframes (115), assessCoverage (271), KEYFRAME_DEFAULTS (40), COVERAGE_THRESHOLDS (261)
**`src/enroll/scale.ts`** — The absolute-scale ladder. `readIris` measures the mean projected iris radius (both eyes, all contour points) and the camera-space depth of the eye-corner plane from the solved pose+geometry; `solveScale` converts those to a per-frame gauge factor (assumedIrisMm / (2·r·Z/f)), takes a median, and reports a relative sigma floored at 0.55/11.70. `applyScale` applies a factor to positions, pose translations and the displacement field together.
  -> readIris (209), solveScale (309), applyScale (431), IRIS (71), PD_PLAUSIBLE_MM (113)
**`src/enroll/detector-bias.ts`** — Holds a per-vertex landmark-vs-skin offset (all zeros today, source 'none', faces 0) that enroll subtracts from the solved surface and facemodel adds back for detector-facing consumers; plus the estimator that would measure it from (reconstruction, truth) pairs, and the scale-free Procrustes used by that estimator and by the test metrics.
  -> detectorBias (82), rigidAlign (189), hornRotation (229), measureDetectorBias (128), loadDetectorBias (93)
**`src/enroll/telemetry.ts`** — NDJSON capture format: header line, then one line per SCAN frame (beat, landmarks 3 dp, sigmaPx, visibility, optional silhouette) and one per WEAR frame (landmarks 4 dp, dt, emitted and raw pose as 12 numbers). Serialises what `enroll/bundle.ts` consumes so a replay runs the shipping path; parse refuses a file whose declared frame counts disagree with its contents.
  -> serializeCapture (168), parseCapture (211), CaptureHeader (80), WearFrame (129)

*Where the difficulty is:*
- **The plateau detector keeps two maxima with one epsilon: `best` (reference, only advances on improvement) and `peak` (true max, drives goal + reporting)** (`src/enroll/protocol.ts:463-499`) — Mis-assigning either turns the stillness test into a per-frame velocity gate (the documented historical bug) and the beat still COMPLETES via `peak >= goal`, so every test that only asserts completion passes. `sinceImprovement` also drives the `settling` UI at 0.4·patience (582).
- **Neutral settlement has three write sites, all gated on `beat.id === 'centre' && neutral === null`** (`src/enroll/protocol.ts:397-404, 442-449, 514-518`) — A neutral latched from a turned pose is silently self-consistent: every later beat measures against it, the nod guards read |dYaw|=50 forever, and the fabricated value is persisted into model.scan and printed by diagnostics. Two of the three sites were fixed independently of the third in history.
- **Two neutrals: `distanceRatio` is computed by the CALLER, yaw/pitch deltas inside `measure()` from `state.neutral`** (`src/enroll/protocol.ts:627 and 233/244 vs 457; caller at src/app/main.ts:1051`) — The lean beats read whatever neutral the caller passed to `sampleFromPose`; the turn/nod beats read the state's. Nothing in protocol.ts can detect a caller that passes null or a stale neutral — lean-in silently becomes unsatisfiable or instantly satisfied.
- **The per-axis extremes block is the only thing making selection span == capture span** (`src/enroll/keyframes.ts:161-178`) — Delete it and farthest-point on a sparse draw loses a motion the wearer performed; the coverage verdict then advises a repeat of a completed beat. The pinning test had to use count=16 because at 24 a broken selector still passes (tests/pipeline.test.ts:1184-1196).
- **assessCoverage mixes two populations: spans come from the SELECTION, hasProfile from ALL frames** (`src/enroll/keyframes.ts:271-283`) — `maxYaw` scans `frames`, including frames rejected by `minLandmarkFraction` and never solved. hasProfile can be true from a frame the bundle never saw; the spans cannot. The two verdicts then feed different consumers (degraded vs a note).
- **Protocol goals and coverage thresholds are unlinked numbers in different files** (`src/enroll/protocol.ts:188/201 (minimum 15, goal 55) vs src/enroll/keyframes.ts:262 (yawS…`) — A reach beat satisfied at its minimum on both sides gives a 30 deg span, below the 50 the coverage gate needs, so a protocol that reports success produces a permanently degraded model. Nothing tests the pair; both are 'measured degrees' of an uncalibrated detector.
- **The iris sigma is dominated by a constant floor, so a bug in its scatter term is invisible** (`src/enroll/scale.ts:372-375`) — `hypot(0.55/11.70, scatter/sqrt(n))` — the second term contributes ~0.02 pp at 24 keyframes, and the <=3-reading fallback (0.05) is a different quantity in the same slot. Any error in the percentile spread never shows in the printed 4.72%.
- **The gauge invariant: everything with length units must scale in one call** (`src/enroll/scale.ts:431-454, applied twice in src/enroll/enroll.ts:194 and :212`) — positions, every pose translation and the displacement field must move together or the model drifts off its own observations; the field was the member that got missed (displacementRmsMm carried pre-scale units under an Mm suffix). The second call (PD correction) multiplies `estimate.factor` but leaves `irisFactor` at its old value (enroll.ts:275-277).
- **The skin/landmark convention split is an identity today, so its sign is untested in production** (`src/enroll/detector-bias.ts:82-91, subtracted at src/enroll/enroll.ts:392-393, added at s…`) — Bias is all zeros, so subtract-then-add and no-op are indistinguishable everywhere except one harness injection (0.6 mm). A sign flip costs ~2 mm of pose error at frontal while reprojection rms moves 0.71→0.94 px — under every gate.
- **hornRotation is a shifted power iteration used to GRADE the estimator** (`src/enroll/detector-bias.ts:229-275, consumed by src/testkit/metrics.ts:65`) — A convergence or shift defect surfaces as huge per-region errors beside sane per-measurement ones — i.e. it reads as an estimator bug, not a grader bug (documented: the polar-decomposition version returned a matrix with a scale factor of several hundred).
- **NaN handling in the capture round-trip is per-field and asymmetric** (`src/enroll/telemetry.ts:184-186 vs 257-262`) — Landmarks and sigmas serialise non-finite as null and parse back to NaN; `visibility` is written with bare `round()` (NaN → JSON null) and read with `Float64Array.from` (null → 0). A missing visibility silently becomes a fully visible one on replay.
- **Replay-fidelity floor is set by three independent rounding choices** (`src/enroll/telemetry.ts:64,77,166`) — Wear landmarks 4 dp, pose R 6 dp, t 4 dp; the replay check compares recorded `emitted` against re-solved poses (scripts/replay-capture.mjs:204). If any of the three is loosened, the check's floor moves and the failure reads as tracker drift.

### src/detect/ — the MediaPipe boundary and landmark uncertainty

**`src/detect/mediapipe.ts`** — Wraps the vendored MediaPipe FaceLandmarker into the tree's one `Detector` interface: builds it (GPU with CPU fallback), forces monotonically increasing VIDEO timestamps, picks one face, and converts normalised landmarks to SOURCE-image pixels as a flat Float64Array. Also holds the boundary's four declared constants (numFaces 2, detect long side 640, roll pre-rotation off, optional confidence gates).
  -> createMediaPipeDetector (116), the returned detect() closure (176-202), build() (138)
**`src/detect/pick-face.ts`** — Chooses which of MediaPipe's detected faces is the wearer by the AREA of its landmark bounding box (area, not width, so a profile view is not handed to a front-on bystander). Returns -1 for no faces, 0 for one.
  -> pickFace (23)
**`src/detect/uncertainty.ts`** — Estimates a per-landmark sigma in pixels from three signals: a floor (floorPx x pixelScale), a quadratic inflation by self-occlusion from rasterising the posed mesh at the PREVIOUS pose, and an EMA of each landmark's residual after a 2D similarity fit of the whole set (frame to frame), combined in quadrature. Also returns the per-vertex visibility the raster produced, which downstream weights observations by.
  -> estimateSigma (145), createUncertainty (98), acquisitionSigma (321), UNCERTAINTY_DEFAULTS (81)

*Where the difficulty is:*
- **`visibility` is a FACING COSINE, not a visibility. `vertexVisibility` returns `max(0,-nz)` for any vertex that passes the depth test (raster.ts:201), so an obliquely visible verte…** (`src/detect/uncertainty.ts:176-185 with src/core/raster.ts:190-202`) — Two different quantities share one name and one array. The same array leaves estimateSigma as `visibility` and is used downstream as an observation WEIGHT (perVertexUncertainty / noseObservations). Any reasoning about occlusion here that assumes 0/1 is wrong, and the confusion is invisible: a frontal hold already runs 4.3x the floor because the mesh rim is permanently oblique (docs/CONSTANTS.md:256).
- **pixelScale is the unit contract for the whole sigma stream, and it is applied to the FLOOR only, in one place.** (`src/detect/uncertainty.ts:151 and src/track/profile.ts:160-176`) — floorPx is calibrated at the 640-px detect resolution; landmarks arrive in source pixels (2x at 1280). Getting it wrong is a factor-4 error in the bundle's weights and it already shipped once (docs/CONSTANTS.md:315). No headless caller can see it — every one runs pixelScale 1 — so the harness returns bit-identical results for changes that alter every real frame. profile.ts:87-105 documents the matching hole that was NOT fixed: maxSigmaPx (12) is never scaled by pixelScale, so the app culls at 8.6 floors where the constant was chosen at 17.
- **NaN is tested on the X coordinate only, in three places, so a landmark with finite x and NaN y poisons `state.disagreement[i]` permanently.** (`src/detect/uncertainty.ts:268, 295-299, 307`) — idx admits the landmark (268 checks only `[i*2]`), r = hypot(NaN,...) = NaN at 295, the EMA at 298 latches NaN for the life of the state, and the final guard at 307 again checks only x so sigma[i] becomes NaN rather than Infinity. NaN sigma is then a divide in the solver, not a cull. Nothing in tests/core.test.ts:1055 covers a half-NaN point.
- **The similarity fit (a,b) is unweighted, unclamped and non-robust by explicit decision.** (`src/detect/uncertainty.ts:271-291`) — a = sa/sp with only `sp > 0` guarding it; a degenerate or near-coincident previous set makes the scale unbounded. Trimming is deliberately refused (comment 214-221) because the second-face fixture is 45-60% intruder, so a genuine second population drags the fit and every residual with it. The `idx.length > 20` threshold at 271 is an undocumented magic number with no CONSTANTS.md row and no test.
- **`state.previous` is the previous CALL's landmarks, not the previous frame's, and the EMA rate assumes per-frame.** (`src/detect/uncertainty.ts:189, 298, 304`) — App skips this whole function on faceless frames (main.ts:948-974 returns before shippedSigma) and on the acquisition branch (profile.ts:161-167 returns acquisitionSigma without touching state.previous). After a dropped detection the disagreement is computed across a multi-frame gap while 0.25 still means ~4 frames of memory. The similarity absorbs in-plane motion but not the out-of-plane leftover, which scales with elapsed rotation — measured 2.53 px at 5.7 deg/frame against the 0.70 floor (comment 234-235).
- **The depth-buffer cache key is four-way and one of its fields is read by nothing.** (`src/detect/uncertainty.ts:164-169`) — It guards width, height, scale and intrinsics IDENTITY, but rasterize takes `input.intrinsics` directly and recomputes s (raster.ts:93); `buffer.intrinsics` is never read anywhere and `buffer.scale` is read only by the zero-caller `extractSilhouette` (raster.ts:222). So a stale buffer is harmless for the fields the test at tests/core.test.ts:1063-1110 is most careful about, while a genuine staleness (in-place mutation of f alone, unchanged dimensions) passes every branch of the key. The test's last arm uses a spread copy, so it is checking referential inequality rather than the guard it names.
- **Occlusion multiplies, disagreement adds in quadrature — the order and the asymmetry are load-bearing and undocumented.** (`src/detect/uncertainty.ts:184 then 299`) — sigma is bounded above by 7x floor from occlusion but the disagreement term is unbounded, so the file's claim that self-occlusion is "the big one" (30-35) is false under motion: identity.ts's own measurement attributes the 1.75-1.97x per-frame excursions to the temporal term, not occlusion (docs/CONSTANTS.md:390). estimateSigma is also non-decreasing from floorPx at every step, an invariant three other modules rely on (identity.ts:478, 625).
- **Landmark index i is assumed to be vertex index i, for i < 468, with the array actually carrying 478.** (`src/detect/mediapipe.ts:196-201, src/detect/uncertainty.ts:145-152 (n = state.vertexCount)`) — mediapipe sizes the array by points.length and declares landmarkCount 478 without asserting it; uncertainty silently ignores everything past vertexCount; enroll/scale.ts:225,244 reads indices 468-477 raw with no length check. A model built without iris refinement produces a 468-landmark array that this boundary accepts and that turns the iris ruler — the only absolute scale in the system — into NaN downstream.
- **Detector timestamp nudge silently changes the frame the model sees.** (`src/detect/mediapipe.ts:180-181`) — `if (timestampMs <= lastTimestamp) timestampMs = lastTimestamp + 1` makes a repeated or rewound source produce a NEW inference on an OLD image rather than the library's repeat. Combined with the previous-frame disagreement in uncertainty.ts this manufactures a zero-motion frame whose EMA pulls disagreement down. Untested — no test imports mediapipe.ts.
- **Per-frame allocation on the hot path.** (`src/detect/uncertainty.ts:152-153, 174, 304 and raster.ts:95-96`) — Every frame allocates sigma, visibility, normals (computeVertexNormals with no `out`), px, depth and a full copy of previous, despite state.normalsCam being preallocated for exactly this purpose (96, 104). Relevant to any pacing measurement (memory: pacing is missed vsyncs, not fps).

### src/track/ — tracker.ts, pnp.ts, profile.ts

**`src/track/pnp.ts`** — The 6-DoF pose solver: POSIT weak-perspective initialisation, then damped-Gauss-Newton (LM) robust refinement on the true perspective model with an optional 6x6 MAP prior fused into the same normal equations. Also computes the two accept/refuse statistics (robust-weighted rmsPx, count-based grossFraction), the a-posteriori variance factor, the pose covariance, and builds Correspondence records (sigma inflation by rigidity, sigma cull, calibration mask).
  -> refinePnP (451), solvePnP (641), posit (268), residualStats (682), buildCorrespondences (796), pixelGateScale (214)
**`src/track/tracker.ts`** — One frame of tracking: builds effective rigidity (static map x visibility smoothstep) and the variance-factor eligibility mask, optionally marches oval landmarks onto the occluding contour, builds a constant-velocity MAP prior, runs warm/cold/basin-audit PnP, applies four refusal gates, then smooths (One Euro / adaptive / stillness latch) and emits a pose plus ~20 diagnostics. Also owns the learned latch rest-floor, the leaky anchor, the release crossfade…
  -> track (1042), createTracker (601), buildMotionPrior (1834), miss (2023), adoptAuditPose (1980), marchStrip (1756), gateFrom (2002), feedFloor (2014)
**`src/track/profile.ts`** — The single definition of the tracker configuration the app ships (smooth/motionPrior/rigidity/adaptiveFloorPx/ovalStrips) and of the per-frame sigma+visibility input, so headless reports and the app run the same system. Pure glue: no numerics of its own.
  -> shippedTrackerOptions (106), shippedSigma (157)

*Where the difficulty is:*
- **The returned covariance is the last iteration's H, accumulated at the PRE-step pose, and (with a prior) is the fused posterior** (`src/track/pnp.ts:596-608`) — H is filled at the top of each iteration (509-540) and the accepted step is applied after (567-576), so C = H(pose_before_last_step)^-1 * varianceFactor. If the loop exits via `!stepped` (582) H is from a rejected iteration; with maxIterations 0 H is all-zero and covariance silently becomes null. This covariance feeds sigmaMm/sigmaDeg, every latch gate lift, the drift guard, and the next frame's motion prior — a one-step staleness the code only admits for the priorShare readout (612-614).
- **Gradient/cost asymmetry for correspondences behind the camera** (`src/track/pnp.ts:521 vs 667`) — In the normal equations a point failing project() is skipped entirely (zero gradient, zero Hessian); in evaluateCost it is charged a flat 1e6. The line search then judges an objective the step cannot reduce — LM escalates lambda and stalls rather than moving the pose to bring the point in front. Invisible on well-conditioned frames; decides behaviour on exactly the degenerate ones the gates exist for.
- **Variance-factor blend mixes a count with a weight sum** (`src/track/pnp.ts:713, 761-770`) — lambda = min(1, inliersCal/VF_CAL_MIN_COUNT) uses a COUNT of lossW>0.5 landmarks while dofCal = 2*wEffCal - 6 uses the WEIGHT SUM over all calibrated ones (including near-zero weights). The two populations diverge as the robust kernel bites, so the blend point and the dof it blends toward move for different reasons. vf scales every covariance this file returns, hence the latch gates and the prior's units.
- **Prior information units: three different frames' variance factors in one matrix** (`src/track/tracker.ts:1951, 1966-1967 and src/track/pnp.ts:605`) — P_last was already multiplied by the PREVIOUS frame's varianceFactor inside refinePnP; buildMotionPrior scales c*P_last, adds Q, inverts, then multiplies by max(vfEma, vfPrev) of the CURRENT frame. A vf regime change makes the prior's claimed strength wrong by the ratio of two frames' factors, indistinguishable downstream from a genuinely confident prediction.
- **The prior grade is a closed loop, and the translation stand-aside is pinned at its floor** (`src/track/tracker.ts:1407-1433, 1930-1931`) — priorMiss is measured from result.pose, the MAP posterior the same prior was fused into, so the residual is shrunk by the prior's own pull. missMm = max(last, EMA, 1) clamps a shipped grade of 0.72-0.93 to 1 while the honest grade is 1.8-3.1 — on roughly half the frames of a hard translational reversal there is effectively NO gate. The most consequential live, documented defect in the area.
- **Prior/reset/velocity/smoother clocks must judge the same darkness at four slightly different thresholds** (`src/track/tracker.ts:1059-1074, 1314-1326, 1461-1463, 1537-1539, 1623`) — gapSeconds is read before the solve; the prior gate uses dtSolve+gap with a 1/30 fallback, the stall reset dt+gap with a 0 fallback, the velocity clock and the smoother add gap, the latch pursuit deliberately does not. Any one drifting out of step reintroduces the double-count / foreshortened-window bugs the comments describe, and no test spans all five.
- **Landmark marching mutates the shared correspondence array in place** (`src/track/tracker.ts:1116-1147`) — `c.vertex = marched` rewrites the index used afterwards by the cold retry (1190), the basin audit (1261), residualStats and the covariance. Two correspondences can land on the SAME model vertex (the frontal remap list at 1124-1127 includes 297->332 while 332 has its own strip); nothing dedups. marchScratch is reset with fill(-1) only inside the if-branch (1147).
- **The gross-fraction fold over sigma-culled landmarks is itself filtered by effective rigidity** (`src/track/tracker.ts:1228-1249 with src/track/pnp.ts:815`) — A culled landmark reaches sigmaCulled only when `!rigidity || rigidity[i] > 0.01`, and `rigidity` is the EFFECTIVE array (static map x visibility ramp, 1080-1088). On a turned head every low-visibility culled landmark drops out of the intruder gate entirely — the gate hardens exactly where the far-side landmarks live, defeating the fold's stated purpose (1209-1219).
- **The latch anchors on the SMOOTHED pose but releases against the RAW pose** (`src/track/tracker.ts:1663 vs 1596-1604, 1625-1641`) — Engage clones `smoothed` (One Euro output, carrying its lag) while drift/velocity release, the pursuit target and the pursuit magnitude all use result.pose. The initial innovation is therefore the filter's steady-state lag, eating part of the LATCH_SLEW_START deadband before any wearer motion. With the guard's 10%/frame contraction floor (1586-1591) this is where 'stuck/choppy' would live — and nothing in the tree engages the latch (221-223).
- **Gate lifts compose a self-sampled learned floor with a per-frame covariance lift by max()** (`src/track/tracker.ts:1555-1591, 2002-2021`) — enter = min(cap, max(learnedFloor, LATCH_GATE_SNR*noiseVel)); noiseVel comes from ring endpoint sigmas over the span, and the floor is learned ONLY from latched frames — the estimator's input distribution depends on its own output (deliberately a ratchet). feedFloor seeds d = v*0.5 from one sample (2017); guardMmLast/DegLast are written on every frame including unlatched ones, so the first latched frame inherits an unlatched guard.
- **POSIT's reference point is correspondences[0], not the centroid** (`src/track/pnp.ts:277-279, 336-343, 373-382`) — The `- x_0` correction (documented at 306-334 as a 74-degree bias when omitted) exists only because the reference is not the centroid. Anything that reorders or filters the correspondence array changes the reference vertex and therefore POSIT's conditioning — including marching, which rewrites vertex indices before the cold retry runs.
- **Two pixel-unit systems that never meet: focal scaling and detect/source scaling** (`src/track/pnp.ts:207-216, 695; src/track/tracker.ts:1175-1176, 1242; src/track/profile.ts…`) — maxRmsPx and GROSS_ERROR_PX scale by min(1, f/587); maxSigmaPx scales by nothing while the sigma floor it is compared against IS scaled by pixelScale (profile.ts:111,164). profile.ts states the resulting ~2x over-aggressive cull as live and ungradeable (every headless caller runs pixelScale 1). That cull is load-bearing for the intruder gate via the fold above.

### src/track/ — smoothing.ts, identity.ts, snap.ts

**`src/track/smoothing.ts`** — One Euro pose filter: six scalar channels (tx/ty/tz through a plain low-pass, rx/ry/rz through a LEAKY-accumulator tangent-space step), each with a measurement-derived speed signal and a decaying-minimum noise floor subtracted from it. Also computes the per-frame `noiseScale` that the `'adaptive'` mode divides the whole cutoff (minCutoff AND the beta term) by.
  -> PoseSmoother.filter (:505), OneEuro.filter (:405), OneEuro.stepDamped (:441), noiseScaleFromSigma (:373), NoiseFloor.observe (:143)
**`src/track/identity.ts`** — A per-frame watch deciding whether the face in front of the camera is still the one the FaceModel was scanned from, using `TrackResult.varianceFactor` as a RATIO to a reference median learned live from the wearer's own first 12 qualifying frames. A separate streak on the mean claimed sigma (the statistic's denominator) retires and relearns the reference when the detector's calibration moves, rather than convicting.
  -> observeIdentity (:550), qualifies (:528), armWearer (:697), forgetWearer (:702), createIdentityWatch (:486)
**`src/track/snap.ts`** — Occlusion-boundary snapping. Extracts the occluding contour from a rasterised depth buffer (interior depth jumps included), walks a ±8 source-px band along each sample's image normal for the strongest luminance-derivative peak, converts confident offsets into face-space millimetre pushes on nearby mesh vertices, and integrates those pushes into a per-vertex CalibrationField (confidence-weighted running mean, Huber agreement weight, rate-limited `applied` …
  -> occludingContour (:241), snapOffsets (:330), contourPushes (:473), CalibrationField.update (:704), CalibrationField.advance (:668), snappedContourPoints (:435)

*Where the difficulty is:*
- **NoiseFloor latches to 0 on the very first observation and takes ~20 s to climb out — so the documented 'measured noise floor subtraction' (file header point 4) is effectively iner…** (`src/track/smoothing.ts:143-154, priming call at :515-517`) — `observe` starts from `Infinity`; on frame 1 `raw` is 0 (`this.last === null`), so `smoothedSpeed` is 0 and the floor is set to 0. Recovery is `a*(speed-floor)*0.15` with `a = 1-exp(-dt/3)` ≈ 0.011 at 30 fps — an effective time constant near 20 s. `effective = speed - noise` is therefore the full speed for hundreds of frames. Invisible: the filter still works, just with a different beta term than the constant comments assume, and the rotation priming at :515 guarantees it on those channels too.
- **The leaky-accumulator damping in `stepDamped` is the numerically delicate core of rotation smoothing and it has NO golden test — only a behavioural overshoot bound.** (`src/track/smoothing.ts:441-463; tests/core.test.ts:1681, 1899`) — `rho = critical + (1-critical)*ROTATION_DAMPING` with `critical = (1-sqrt a)/(1+sqrt a)` is a closed form asserted only in a comment (:456-459); a reviewer must re-derive it from `z^2 - (1+b-a)z + b`. The file states at :436-437 that no golden reaches here, and the 'rotation' golden at tests/core.test.ts:1913 runs `OneEuro.filter` with ROTATION_SMOOTHING settings — the translation path. A sign or ordering error in `stepDamped` still passes the ≤5% overshoot test.
- **`contourPushes` gathers vertices purely by 2D image distance with no visibility or depth test, so a back-facing vertex projecting near the contour is pushed as if it were on the s…** (`src/track/snap.ts:502-515`) — The gather is `Math.hypot(px[v]-s.x, py[v]-s.y) <= gatherPx`; `pz[v]` is used only for the mm conversion at :524, never to reject. At yaw the far-side mesh vertices project into the same 6-px disc as the near silhouette, take the near sample's offset, and their corrections are then frozen into `CalibrationField` for the session. No fixture in tests/snap.test.ts:264-311 has a back side.
- **The 53% flank population — which the module's own header calls pure invention at 3.5-5.4 mm median |offset| and 23-45% of every confident sample — is documented at length and test…** (`src/track/snap.ts:143-201`) — The second-difference separator was measured and rejected (:163-189), so the shipped code has no defence against flanks at all. Every test fixture is either a real edge or flat/noisy skin. A change that made flank acceptance better or worse would move no test. Largest known error source in the file, entirely un-instrumented.
- **`CalibrationField` freezing is asymptotic, not a latch, and `convergence()` is monotone by construction.** (`src/track/snap.ts:704-733, :737-747`) — `gain = c/(w+c)` uses the PRE-cap weight, so once `w == weightCap` the gain floors at `c/(15+c)` ≈ 0.0625 per frame — sustained biased input still walks the estimate fully, while the docs say 'FROZEN' (:556-557) and 'at most a sixteenth' (main.ts:1713). Separately `weight` only increases, so `convergence()` can never fall; main.ts:1209 drops to 1-in-8 snap frames on `convergence() > 0.9` with no route back short of `adoptModel` nulling the field (main.ts:1718).
- **The identity watch's calibration rests entirely on `varianceFactor`'s denominator, and the drift guard's bar is an AGGREGATE applied per-frame through a streak.** (`src/track/identity.ts:642-661`) — `sigmaScale = obs.meanSigmaPx / watch.referenceSigma` compares one frame against a 12-frame median, while IDENTITY_SIGMA_DRIFT_MAX = 1.6 was derived session-half against session-half (:446-463). The file admits (:465-475) the per-frame quantity reaches 1.75-1.97x in 8 of 8 same-person sessions. The rise/fall asymmetry rests wholly on `estimateSigma` being non-decreasing from `floorPx` — an invariant that lives in detect/uncertainty.ts, not here. If it stops holding, the fall branch false-convicts on one frame.
- **Retirement destroys strictly more state than a conviction, and the relearn window is a documented open door onto a stranger.** (`src/track/identity.ts:650-659 vs :680-685`) — 'recalibrating' clears reference, referenceSigma, learning, learningSigma, recent AND strikes; a conviction clears only `recent` and `strikes` and KEEPS `reference`. So a drift arriving with a swap relearns the reference from the stranger (:133-141), and a sustained excursion guarantees the relearn window opens inside the excursion (:608-617). Both stated costs, not bugs — but the two branches leave the watch in incomparable states.
- **`occludingContour`'s `half` boundary shift is a conditioning correction whose correct value depends on the reported normal's orientation, and its output is frozen into the wearer'…** (`src/track/snap.ts:279-284; analysis at :126-141 and :203-214`) — `half = 0.5*max(|nx|,|ny|)` is derived from which 4-neighbour fires first, so axis-aligned normals are under-shifted (+0.53 mm) and diagonal ones over-shifted (-0.46 mm). This lands as scan error through `contourPushes` and is integrated into `CalibrationField` as a property of the face (:76-82). Any change to normal estimation silently changes the correct `half` — the header records an 8-neighbour Sobel moving the mean right and the mean-absolute wrong.
- **snapOffsets' band-end path bypasses the parabola and emits exactly ±searchPx — the largest offset the module can produce — at above-median confidence.** (`src/track/snap.ts:374-401, :403-413`) — The one-sided ridge gate (added 2026-08-26) is the only thing between a noise spike at a band end and a full-cap 3 mm push in a direction noise chose; the header records 30 of 42 band-end peaks accepted at grain ±8 before it existed. `interior` gates both the ridge test AND the sub-pixel refinement, so band-end accepts have zero sub-pixel resolution by design.
- **`PoseSmoother`'s rotation channels reuse `OneEuro.last` for a different quantity than the translation channels do, with no guard.** (`src/track/smoothing.ts:392-394, :407 vs :444`) — In `filter` `last` holds the absolute measurement; in `stepDamped` it holds the innovation (angle from the previous OUTPUT). They never mix today only because tx/ty/tz call one and rx/ry/rz the other; `accumulator` is likewise touched only by `stepDamped`. Any future caller mixing them on one instance gets a silently wrong speed signal.

### src/fit/ — the contact seat solver (contact.ts)

**`src/fit/contact.ts`** — Solves where a frame comes to rest on a personal face model by minimising a 6-DOF energy E = one-sided pad contact + gravity + one-sided ear support + forward hook + clearance + weak pose prior, using damped Levenberg-Marquardt with a Gauss-Newton Hessian and gravity folded into the gradient only. Then re-walks the settled pose to report contact diagnostics (pad load share, depth/flushness, pad tilt and unfixable curvature, pantoscopic/roll, vertex distan…
  -> solveSeat (577) → energyTerms (724) / accumulate (803) → describeSeat (1244) → padArticulation (1514); geometry helpers earRestPoints (1141), nominalPose (1174), landmarkHungPose (1203), clearanceSamples (1064); constants SKIN (127), SEAT_DEFAULTS (318), TARGET_CONTACT_MM (445), PAD_CURVATURE_LIMIT…

*Where the difficulty is:*
- **energyTerms and accumulate are two independent transcriptions of the same energy; any drift silently makes LM step on one objective and accept by another** (`src/fit/contact.ts:724 vs :803`) — This exact class of bug already shipped twice here (the cp.n-vs-u contact row, and the clearance stiffness divided in one and not the other). Only the pad/ear/hook/gravity/prior paths are differenced by tests/core.test.ts:1442 and :1574, both with clearance=null or t.clearance===0, so the clearance rows in both functions have NO gradient coverage at all.
- **Penetration-branch magnitude cap exists in accumulate but not in energyTerms** (`src/fit/contact.ts:820 (maxMagnitude 30, pads) vs :735-751 (no cap)`) — A pad sample more than 30 mm from the nose+cheeks field contributes to the energy but contributes zero gradient/Hessian — a real energy/derivative mismatch that no test can reach with the parametric frames, and that a scanned GLB with stray pad samples (frame-from-mesh) could reach.
- **The contact row's direction u = (p−cp)/|p−cp| and the reported vertical lift −u_y must stay opposite signs** (`src/fit/contact.ts:844-853 (row builds +u), :1309 (lift uses (cp.y − p[1]) = −u_y)`) — Force = −∇E = −k·pen·u, so the report's minus sign is correct only given accumulate's +u row. Flipping either alone is silent: the docstring records that a naive +u_y drove padLoadFraction to 0.0% on all 145 catalogue pairs while the solve was unchanged. tests/pipeline.test.ts:1556 is the only guard and it holds only in the all-samples-bearing regime.
- **The rotational prior's gradient is a first-order approximation of d/dw of 0.5·k·|log(R·Rprior^T)|²** (`src/fit/contact.ts:947-951 (g += k·w, H += k) against energyTerms:754-756`) — Exact is k·J_r^{-T}w; the code uses k·w. The smooth-regime gradient test (tests/core.test.ts:1499+) only perturbs translation from an identity-rotation prior, so w = 0 and the approximation is exact there; the contact-regime test (:1574) has rotation but only bounds gradient ANGLE < 5 deg and magnitude ratio 0.95-1.05, which cannot see an O(|w|²) error. No test can fail on this.
- **padSeatErrorMm and padDepthErrorMm are pooled across BOTH pads from one `signed` array** (`src/fit/contact.ts:1263, :1398-1408`) — An asymmetric seat (left pad buried, right hovering) cancels in the mean, so padDepthErrorMm reads ~0 while the flushness number absorbs the asymmetry. The per-side split exists (`per[-1]`/`per[1]`) but is not used for these two headline numbers. pipeline.test.ts:1616 grades |padDepthErrorMm| and would be blind to the same cancellation.
- **padLoadFraction's denominator is pads+ears only; the approach spring, the prior and clearance forces are outside the vertical balance** (`src/fit/contact.ts:1338-1351, :1379`) — PAD_APPROACH_FRACTION (573) adds a real downward pull on hovering pads that appears in neither padLift nor earLift, and the comment at :1355-1378 already concedes the prior puts 1-17% of the frame's weight through the pads on perched seats. So padOverClosure > 1 is diagnosed in prose but the balance itself is knowingly incomplete.
- **The LM termination logic makes the pure-evaluator mode report converged:false, always** (`src/fit/contact.ts:623 (loop), :666 (`if (iterations >= opt.maxIterations)`)`) — With maxIterations:0 the loop body never runs, iterations stays 0, 0 >= 0 is true, so terminationReason = 'iteration budget' and converged stays false. Every harness control (report-seat.ts:300, pipeline.test.ts:1583/1634/1642) runs in exactly this mode, so a reviewer comparing `converged` across controls and real solves is comparing an artefact.
- **capStep(dx, 4, 0.12) truncates the LM step before the accept test, silently changing the trust region** (`src/fit/contact.ts:641, capStep at :990`) — A capped direction is still the LM direction but the lambda schedule (:648, :654) is tuned assuming the untruncated step. Combined with the one-sided active set this is what determines whether the solve tunnels into the wrong local minimum; nothing measures how often the cap actually binds.
- **padArticulation's detrend basis is built from the MEAN PAD NORMAL, with a hand-rolled degenerate-case ladder** (`src/fit/contact.ts:1591-1626 (seed selection at :1595-1597, determinant fallback at :1617…`) — This is the estimator PAD_CURVATURE_LIMIT_MM is thresholded against, and its predecessor (index-regression) reported 1.084 mm on an exactly planar field. The rank-deficient fallback (`saa >= sbb`) picks ONE axis; the residual then carries n−3 DOF (:1635) even though only 2 parameters were fitted, biasing the residual DOWN on collinear pads. pipeline.test.ts:1711 only checks order-invariance, not correctness on a synthetic curved field.
- **The whole fore-aft answer hangs on two stated scalars in earRestPoints, and the ear/hook terms are the only thing opposing gravity and the sidewall's +0.60 forward push** (`src/fit/contact.ts:1122 (EYE_TO_TRAGION_Z_MM = 73), :1139 (ABOVE_EYE_LINE_MM = 6), used a…`) — 73 was swept, not derived (69 vs 77 moves signed padDepth 1.49 → 0.53 mm and panto 6.2 → 0.9 deg per the docstring's own table). The docstring says to re-sweep if temple reach, hinge convention or pad geometry changes — none of which the code can detect. Note earRestPoints is ALSO the occluder's ear input (src/app/main.ts:1747, tests/head.test.ts:242), so the same two constants span fit and occlusion.
- **Face-space rotation is about the FACE ORIGIN, not the frame or a contact point** (`src/fit/contact.ts:963-969 (pointJacobian) with poseOplus at src/core/linalg.ts:296 (left…`) — Convention is internally consistent (d/dw = −skew(R·p)), but it strongly couples the rotation and translation columns of H at the ~60-90 mm lever arm of the ear rests, which is a large part of why the damped step needs the 0.12 rad cap. A reviewer checking pointJacobian against a body-frame convention will 'find' a sign error that is not there.
- **solveSeat rebuilds a whole-face MeshDistance on every call because useClearance defaults true, for a term that provably never engages** (`src/fit/contact.ts:318 (SEAT_DEFAULTS), :601-603, and the dead-term evidence at :244-258`) — This is the expensive half of a seat solve and it is paid on every frame change in the app (src/app/main.ts:1909, synchronous, on the phase-adoption path). Any perf finding here is about a term that cannot fire.

*Where the difficulty is:*
- **derivePads runs on positions that have been rotated and scaled but NOT translated: the central-column test `Math.abs(cx) > halfWidth` (default 18 mm) assumes the file's x origin i…** (`src/fit/frame-asset.ts:834,902 — called from src/fit/frame-from-mesh.ts:1025, before the …`) — An asset authored off-midline in x, or one whose orient leaves a residual x offset, has the column cut through one side. PAD_SIDE_IMBALANCE_MAX is documented as the catcher (glasses01 reads 0.415) but 0.15 passes a modest offset while still biasing separation and the pad origin. No test constructs an x-offset asset.
- **Two independent unit-scale defaults that agree only by convention: readGlb(scaleToMm=1000) and frameFromMesh(options.meshScaleToMm=1000)** (`src/fit/mesh-io.ts:123 and src/fit/frame-from-mesh.ts:992,1263`) — meshToFrame = mul4(toFrame, uniformScale(meshScaleToMm)) is correct only if the caller passed the same factor to readGlb. Nothing checks. readGlb(bytes,1) plus default frameFromMesh yields a correct solve and a frame drawn 1000x off — exactly the fit/render divergence FrameSource exists to prevent.
- **signedVolume winding check sums over ALL triangles of the whole asset about the world origin, on meshes that are usually open shells and multi-part** (`src/fit/frame-asset.ts:861-871`) — For a non-closed mesh this is not a winding invariant; it merely comes out positive on all ten shipped assets. A partially-inverted or shell-dominated asset passes silently — and an inverted asset that passes makes derivePads find the BACK of each pad with a plausible separation.
- **deriveArmRest's two-segment break search and guard chain: overlapping segments, a compacted-bin index, and a fall test extrapolated from the fitted slope** (`src/fit/frame-from-mesh.ts:604-608 (loop), 613 (curlFallMm), 629 (ratio)`) — fit(0,k) and fit(k,len-1) share bin k, biasing the residual minimum; `best` indexes the compacted zs array (empty bins dropped) so the break's geometric meaning moves with bin occupancy; curlFallMm is extrapolated, not measured. The documented margins (nearest asset 8.0x the bar) mean none of this is observable on the catalogue — a bug here is invisible to every green test.
- **The three-tier ear-rest precedence, which sets the highest-leverage number in the tree (±5 mm of reach carries the corneal vertex across the whole 12-16 mm band)** (`src/fit/frame-from-mesh.ts:1046-1140`) — findBend must succeed on BOTH sides or the asset drops to the knee fit (1052-1054); the knee must be believable on both sides or it drops to a wearer-derived constant. navigator's two paths disagree by ~8.3 mm of reach (documented 536-541), so tier precedence, not measurement, pins the calibration asset.
- **findBend's level-run walk is anchored on topBin, the highest binned mean, ties resolved to the REARMOST bin** (`src/fit/frame-from-mesh.ts:820-856`) — The forward walk exists only to patch that tie behaviour; the level-run fraction is then bins over the part's own z span, and empty bins are skipped in the walks yet still counted in (levelFront-bend)/bins*span. A temple with a gap reads a longer level run than it has.
- **PAD_CONTACT_CONE_COS narrowing keeps the best PAD_MIN_FACES rather than refusing, applied twice (narrow, re-mean, narrow)** (`src/fit/frame-asset.ts:1008-1023`) — On a highly curved pad the reported separation, yaw and samples come from a 20-face sliver, continuously and with no flag — the derivation cannot say it degraded, and everything downstream (origin, separation, padAngleRad) is taken off these contact faces (1030-1032).
- **padAngleRad is a YAW (atan2(|nz|,|nx|)) on both production paths while PAD_INWARD_COS gates on a CONE angle from the x axis** (`src/fit/frame-asset.ts:1085-1090 vs 557-600, gate at 907`) — Two different angles about the same pad, differing 6.7-8.5 deg on real assets and IDENTICALLY equal on parametric frames (ny==0). Every round-trip test built on parametric frames is blind to a re-confusion of the two.
- **rimHalfAxes' clamp order: the floor wins over the hinge cap** (`src/fit/frame-layout.ts:141-148`) — `Math.max(floor, Math.min(inner, outerCap))` lets a narrow-front asset whose outerCap falls below 0.11*frontWidthMm get rims extending past the hinge, silently — and this geometry is what report-occlusion samples and frame-geometry draws.
- **Aliased Float64Arrays in the returned FrameLayout: rims[s].centre and lenses[s].centre are the same object** (`src/fit/frame-layout.ts:158-159, 186-187`) — `readonly` is field-level only; a consumer that transforms a layout in place would move rim and lens disc together. Nothing does today, so nothing would catch the first one that does.
- **thinPads strides a face list whose spatial coherence is assumed, per side, rounding each side's share independently** (`src/fit/frame-from-mesh.ts:946-980`) — The two `want` values need not sum to budget, and on a scan with incoherent triangle order the retained 64 samples can be a clustered subset of the pad, shifting the contact centroid the seat integrates over. The only documented defence is that the budget was adopted on the seated result.
- **The pad-centroid origin is defined by the SELECTED contact faces, and every later measurement is expressed in it** (`src/fit/frame-from-mesh.ts:1028-1042`) — Any change inside derivePads' selection moves the origin, hence reach, height, vertex distance and meshToFrame for all ten mesh assets at once. No test pins the origin against an independent reference.

*Where the difficulty is:*
- **The flip lives on exactly one graph edge, and nothing mechanically enforces it** (`src/render/convert.ts:67 and :94; src/render/scene.ts:440, :609, :498`) — poseToGLMatrix is legal ONLY on headNode; frameNode (a child of headNode) and the occluder's vertex buffer must use the unflipped path. The only mechanised check is tests/core.test.ts:1289-1300, comparing the two products for one hand-made pose.
- **nudgeOccluder mutates a live GPU buffer with three overlapping length assumptions and never recomputes normals** (`src/render/scene.ts:548-579`) — `n = min(a.length, occluderBase.length, deltaMm.length)` (:555) defines the face/skull boundary by array length rather than by occluderShell.faceVertexCount, which IS in scope two lines later (:575). If deltaMm ever exceeds 3*faceVertexCount the loop overwrites lofted skull vertices, and the reloft only restores from faceVertexCount*3 onward — a partial overlap is unrecoverable and invisible. Separately, computeVertexNormals runs once in setOccluder (:500) and NEVER after a nudge, while key.shadow.normalBias = 6 mm (:287) and the catcher's receiveShadow both consume those normals.
- **Occluder and shadow catcher share one geometry INSTANCE, and the shadow depends on that identity** (`src/render/scene.ts:494-542`) — catcher = new Mesh(geometry, ...) with the same object (:540). A clone is value-identical until the first measured face, then the depth test culls the shadow entirely. Disposal therefore must collect into Sets (:470-478) or it double-frees a GPU buffer. - **The principal-point shear is dormant, unverifiable in production, and was previously inverted** (`src/render/scene.ts:380-434, src/render/convert.ts:154-159`) — principalPointOffset returns null on every shipped path (every `pp` mask is false), so the branch has never executed on a real session. The shipped code was `projectionMatrix.elements[8] += offset.x`, wrong by 2x in the wrong direction; the fix uses setViewOffset(-dx,-dy). The sign argument rests on three's makePerspective putting the optical axis at NDC -te[8]. Only a stub camera (tests/scene.test.ts:562-585) records the call — no real three.js ever evaluates it.
- **applyIntrinsics is the only thing that sets camera.aspect; setSize does not** (`src/render/scene.ts:332-334 vs :382`) — aspect comes from intrinsics.width/height, the drawing-buffer size from setSize. If the two ever disagree (source-shape change ordering, F29's adoptSourceSize path) the composite stretches against the video with no error and no test — and the frame slides off the face in exactly the way a tracking bug does.
- **orientAlong builds a basis from a hardcoded up vector with no degeneracy guard** (`src/render/frame-geometry.ts:76-86`) — `x = (0,1,0) × z` is the zero vector for a vertical run; normalize() then yields NaN and the mesh quaternion is NaN. Bridge/endpiece/temple runs are near-horizontal today, but nothing bounds a future asset's endpoints. tests/layout.test.ts's Matrix4 stub is a no-op (:118), so orientation is untested end to end — only the midpoint position is checked.
- **Dispose ownership is split across two files and one string flag** (`src/render/scene.ts:645, :657-671, :679; src/render/frame-geometry.ts:203-226`) — CACHED_BY_CALLER on userData decides whether a swapped-out child is destroyed. Set it wrongly on a parametric frame and you leak (v1's bug); forget it on a cached glTF group and the app's cache holds a group whose geometry and textures are gone — draws nothing, silently, and only for already-visited frames. envMap is deliberately excluded from TEXTURE_SLOTS because three assigns scene.environment into material.envMap; disposing it there would kill the environment for everything after.
- **loadFrameMesh applies makeLens to EVERY material on a lens-named node** (`src/render/frame-mesh.ts:154-161`) — `isLens` is a per-NODE predicate (node name OR any material name matches /lens/i), but the inner loop rewrites transmission, ior, roughness, metalness and thickness on all of that node's materials. A multi-material mesh whose slot 0 is a lens and slot 1 the rim turns the rim into glass. The header's zero-false-positive measurement was over names, not over slot fan-out.
- **setHeadPose allocates two Float32Array(16) per frame despite the scratch comment** (`src/render/scene.ts:439, :459 vs the comment at :241`) — `headWorld` is the only scratch. `m` and `mb` are fresh allocations on the 30-60 Hz path, which is exactly what the comment claims does not happen — the kind of contradiction that makes a reviewer trust the wrong line when hunting GC pauses.
- **Un-gated numeric constants: the entire lighting and shadow rig** (`src/render/scene.ts:271-323, :538; src/render/frame-geometry.ts:97-110`) — scripts/check-constants.mjs only sees EXPORTED names. Key intensity 1.2, screen 0.35, ambient 0.35, shadow radius 5, bias -0.0002, normalBias 6 mm, frustum ±250/50/3000, KEY_OFFSET_MM (300,600,1000), PMREM 0.04, ShadowMaterial opacity 0.18, camera near 10 / far 5000, acetate 0.32/0.06, lens opacity 0.13 — none is in docs/CONSTANTS.md and none can be. Only OCCLUDER_BIAS_MM, LENS_IOR and LENS_ROUGHNESS carry ledger rows.
- **createScene's dispose() releases almost nothing it owns** (`src/render/scene.ts:585-594`) — It disposes the renderer, the background texture and the environment texture, and leaves the occluder geometry, both occluder materials and everything under frameNode allocated. There is no test on dispose() at all.
- **Two files author 'a lens' with opposite depth policies** (`src/render/frame-geometry.ts:108 (depthWrite:false, transparent:true) vs src/render/frame…`) — frame-mesh's header argues at length that a lens which writes no depth cannot be occluded by the nose — the whole reason the occluder exists. *Where the difficulty is:*
- **The pixels/pose/timestamp triple is only two-thirds locked on the camera path** (`src/app/sources.ts:118-133 with src/app/framelock.ts:156-166`) — rVFC stores the LAST metadata in `pending`; nextFrame returns it with `source: video`, and framelock draws whatever the element presents at drawImage time. A frame presenting between callback and draw makes capturedAtMs/timestampMs describe older pixels. Silent: mirrorDelayMs reads long, captureDt (the dt driving tracker velocity and the motion prior) is off by one interval, and MediaPipe VIDEO mode gets a timestamp for different pixels.
- **detect->source scale is a single scalar over an independently rounded canvas** (`src/app/framelock.ts:135-137 and :220-221`) — detect.width/height are rounded separately from `shrink`, but detectToSourceScale returns capture.width/detect.width only and scaleLandmarksToSource applies it to x and y alike. Exact at 1280x720/640; anisotropic at other shapes (phone rotation, the 1024x1024 still). Feeds every landmark, therefore PnP depth and the bundle.
- **Two pixel-space conventions meet in the sigma stream and only one is scaled** (`src/app/main.ts:730-739 and :999-1003; src/track/profile.ts:157-177`) — shippedSigma scales the acquisition floor by pixelScale (source px) while shippedTrackerOptions' maxSigmaPx is unscaled (main.ts:715-719 admits it). - **The scanGen / superseded() invariant spans four files and three await points** (`src/app/main.ts:1445-1546 (gen :1449, superseded :1475-1485, checks :1487/:1503/:1541); b…`) — runEnrollment snapshots frames/source/size/PD before a paint yield and must re-check both generation AND source identity+size at every resumption. Miss one and an abandoned solve adopts a model, stamps it with the restarted protocol's scan record and writes it to localStorage. Only reproducible with a mid-scan renegotiation or a click during the solve.
- **app.lastPose lifetime must track the tracker's internal lastRaw exactly** (`src/app/main.ts:1112`) — `tracked.rawPose ?? (app.tracker?.lastRaw ? app.lastPose : null)` decides whether the next frame rasterises visibility against a live or stale pose and whether refinePnP gets a warm start the tracker just refused. It reaches into TrackerState.lastRaw — a cross-module invariant with tracker.ts's lostSecondsBeforeReset (0.5 s) that no test can see.
- **Two clocks with a hand-argued 'exactly one scheduler' proof** (`src/app/main.ts:598-652 (startWatchdog :604, resumeRaf :626, step :634)`) — Correctness rests on tick never scheduling (:801), the rAF chain not re-arming once usingTimer, resumeRaf clearing `fallback` before the flip, and document.hidden gating the latch. A regression presents as high fps and dropped frames, not an error — the historical failure was exponential rAF fan-out reading as '10,000 fps'.
- **snapRaster's cache key is Intrinsics object IDENTITY** (`src/app/main.ts:1188-1200`) — app.intrinsics is reassigned from four sites (:706, :1617, :1733, adoptSourceSize). Identity comparison is correct only because those sites allocate; an in-place mutation would reuse a buffer built for another projection and the wear-phase occluder would drift with nothing logging.
- **contourLuminance bridges three pixel spaces in one closure** (`src/app/main.ts:1256-1312 (toDisp at :1291)`) — Contour samples are INTRINSICS px, the readback box is DISPLAY canvas px, landmarks are source px. - **scanSilhouette rasterises the TEMPLATE and reads a display canvas whose contents depend on lock.present having succeeded** (`src/app/main.ts:1367-1378, ordering at :894-896`) — present() returns false on epoch mismatch, leaving `display` holding an older frame while the contour comes from this frame's pose. Stable within one tick today, so it cannot fire — but the coupling is undocumented, and abstention vs wrong-edge both look like 'fewer points' downstream.
- **Frame-swap ordering: attachFrame before applySeat, and fitFrame must stay synchronous** (`src/app/main.ts:1906-1924 with adoptModel :1755-1761`) — applySeat writes frameNode.matrix and refreshes world matrices over the child attachFrame just added; adoptModel sets phase='wear' on the next line. - **Identity conviction is a four-term predicate assembled across two files, and it is destructive** (`src/app/main.ts:1149-1159, armWearer :1531, re-arm :756`) — Armed only after a live-camera scan; called only for a non-degraded model in wear; meanSigmaPx must go through meanFinite (:1867) or Infinity from hidden landmarks fires the drift guard on every turn. - **collectFrame's halving eviction interacts with the keyframe selector's own choice** (`src/app/main.ts:1406-1443 (COLLECT_BUDGET 240, :1441)`) — Repeated halving decimates by 2^k, with the temporal-spread argument made only in prose. *Where the difficulty is:*
- **Iris synthesis: fixation slerp, prior pull, and one shared displacement** (`src/testkit/synthetic.ts:1000-1224`) — The only absolute ruler in the tree. `hidden` comes from `discFacing` gated by `regionSeen` (1130-1132), the prior disc reuses the SUBJECT's disc orientation (1174), and the inflation is one per-eye displacement added to centre and all four contour points (1154-1155) precisely so it cancels in `readIris`'s mean-norm radius. Any change that makes the rim scatter per-point manufactures a ~+18% one-directional scale bias; any change that makes the prior face-on invents foreshortening. Both are invisible in every printed column except scale%.
- **RNG stream identity is the harness's whole validity, and salts are structural** (`src/testkit/synthetic.ts:242-271, 660; random.ts:46-48`) — A collapsed salt makes every scalar figure common-mode and cannot be seen in any region-RMS column (the file documents the 51-cells-into-6-streams incident). Only `assertDistinctNoiseStreams` catches it, and only if a caller passes the whole grid. Conversely, any edit that adds or removes an rng draw per frame silently re-rolls every committed number — `wanderScale` multiplies the drawn value (728-729) specifically to avoid that, while `gazeAmplitudeMm > 0` (738) does not.
- **Iris ethnic group is a function of the subject SLOT, not of the seed** (`src/testkit/synthetic.ts:198-199`) — `HVID_GROUP_MEANS[index % 4]` means campaign seeds re-draw shape and noise but never re-draw the group assignment; slot 0 is always 11.10. Any 'across seeds' claim about ruler/scale error is replicating one fixed ethnicity assignment.
- **`distribution` poisons to NaN on any non-finite, and every report funnels through it** (`src/testkit/metrics.ts:172-186`) — Deliberate, but it makes each printed cell all-or-nothing and it composes: `report-occlusion`'s `median()` helper (1000) pools thousands of per-pixel values, so one NaN pixel takes an entire headline row to NaN. Also `worst` is max of |v| while `median`/`p90` are of raw signed values (181) — `fmt` prints them side by side.
- **The depth-gauge normalisation is what makes any occlusion depth comparison meaningful** (`src/testkit/report-occlusion.ts:327-347`) — `gauge = truthPose.t[2] / fit.pose.t[2]` scales geometry AND translation AND the sample cloud (933). It is a similarity about the camera centre, so it must leave projected pixels fixed; if the sample cloud were not scaled by the same gauge (scaleSamples), metric B's depth comparison would silently drift while metric A stayed clean.
- **stabilityRun duplicates synthesizeCapture's per-frame path by hand** (`src/testkit/report-occlusion.ts:718-773`) — Wander coefficients 0.25/0.3/0.8/0.25, the ±14 mm clamp, bias sigma 0.6 and noise 0.7 are retyped literals, not imports from CAPTURE_DEFAULTS. It also ignores `wanderScale` entirely, so metric C cannot be run at the measured 0.15 the rest of the tree now has. A change to the capture model moves three reports and not this one.
- **Metric A returns silence, not error, when the occluder contour is empty** (`src/testkit/report-occlusion.ts:527-528`) — `boundaryPx` is sized `occBand.length ? truthBand.length/2 : 0`. An occluder that projects no in-band contour at all contributes zero samples to the pooled median instead of a large error — the failure mode most likely for a badly wrong arm produces the best-looking column.
- **Truth and occluder can have different topologies but the crop and the band do not know it** (`src/testkit/report-occlusion.ts:445-485`) — `armIndices`/`armVertices` let an arm carry its own triangles (head shell), but the crop bbox loops only `mesh.vertexCount` of the truth arm (457) and `hiddenEps`/band padding are computed from `truth.pose.t[2]` (509). Correct only because buildHeadShell keeps face vertices first at their own indices — an invariant enforced in core/head.ts, not here.
- **The seat controls' honesty depends on maxIterations:0 + explicit initialPose** (`src/testkit/report-seat.ts:181-225, 295-304`) — `evaluateSeatOn` turns solveSeat into a pure evaluator; if solveSeat ever ignores `maxIterations: 0` or re-initialises when `initialPose` is present, all three controls silently become 'solve against the right nose' and pass by looking like the baseline — the exact zero-by-construction failure the header is written against.
- **`STILL_BEATS` is derived from the beat table at module load with CAPTURE_DEFAULTS** (`src/testkit/report-track.ts:228-233`) — It is computed once from `protocolBeats(CAPTURE_DEFAULTS)` — not from the beats the run actually synthesised. A run with different capture options (e.g. includeLean/includeProfile off, or a variant turnYawDeg) would test membership against the default beat set; today it happens to resolve to `centre` alone either way.
- **Rejection sampling bounds only the sampled subjects** (`src/testkit/synthetic.ts:154-181`) — `namedExtreme` appends two faces without any `isPlausible` check, and their coefficients are ±2.0/2.2 on four correlated nose modes. Every 'population' figure includes two faces guaranteed to be outside the sampled distribution, and `report-occlusion` weights them heavily (2 of the 3 pooled-iris subjects, 2 of 6 in replication seeds).
- **Two silhouette extractors with different border policies** (`src/testkit/synthetic.ts:1227-1242 vs src/core/raster.ts:219`) — `extractSil` skips the 1-pixel border ring; `extractSilhouette` (used by occlusionCell) does not, which is why occlusionCell needs its own crop margin (469). A change to either alone desynchronises what 'contour' means between enrollment input and occlusion measurement.

## 8. METHOD AND RULES

1. **Read the code. Never trust a comment, a docstring, a README, or this prompt.**
   Everything here is a starting point and some of it is wrong.
2. **Cite `file:line` for every claim.** A finding without a line number is an opinion.
3. **Distinguish what you verified from what you inferred.** Use the confidence field
   honestly. "I could not determine this" is a valid and useful output; a confident wrong
   finding costs more than a missing one.
4. **Run things.** `npm run check` is fast. `npm test` is a minute. The reports are
   runnable. If a claim is checkable by execution, check it rather than reasoning about it.
5. **Do not fix anything.** This is a review. Proposed fixes belong in the finding, as a
   sentence, not as a diff.
6. **Do not stop at a budget.** If you run out of room, say which directories you covered
   completely and which you did not, rather than thinning coverage everywhere.
7. **Report the good too**, briefly. If a module is genuinely sound, say so in one line
   and move on — I need to know where NOT to spend effort as much as where to spend it.
8. **Never invent a measurement.** If you state a number, either it came from a file you
   read, or from a command you ran and can quote. Do not estimate and present it as
   measured; this project has been burned by exactly that more than once.
