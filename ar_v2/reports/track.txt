WHAT THE FILTER COSTS AND BUYS
------------------------------
The lag is visible above as the gap between v2 and v2-no-smoothing on
total error; the three lines under each arm are what that lag buys.
Both belong in the same report — reporting only the cost is how a
filter gets tuned to nothing, and reporting only the benefit is how it
gets tuned until the frame swims.

All three difference the ERROR, not the estimate:

  crawl (all frames)   |e_t - e_{t-1}|, every consecutive pair inside
                       one beat. What the wearer sees as the frame
                       moving against the face.
  crawl (still beats)  the same, restricted to the beats whose pose
                       does not move at all, so a verdict cannot be
                       blamed on the wider window. Derived from the
                       beat table: the retired column counted two
                       beats that ramp 6 degrees of roll as holds.
  shimmer              |e_{t+1} - 2e_t + e_{t-1}|. A pure delay against
                       a smooth trajectory barely registers here, so
                       this is the axis the filter was adopted for.

The column these replace differenced the ESTIMATE against its own
previous frame, so the wearer's postural wander — around a millimetre
per frame, hold beats included — was counted as jitter and a lagging
filter won by not following it. See the `crawl` field comment.

Note that this protocol is a deliberately fast sweep (35 degrees in a
third of a second); ordinary browsing is slower and the lag is
correspondingly smaller. The harness also runs without the motion
prior, which the app switches on by default.
