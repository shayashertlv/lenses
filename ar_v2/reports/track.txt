THE COMPLAINT, QUANTIFIED
-------------------------
"The glasses are pushed forward at >40 degrees" is a statement about how
much the depth error CHANGES with yaw — a constant offset would be
invisible, because the wearer never sees the frame anywhere else.

arm              depth err at 0 deg  depth err when turned  swing mm
---------------  ------------------  ---------------------  --------
v2                             0.44                   1.99      1.56
average-head                   5.28                   0.27      5.01
v2-no-smoothing                0.14                   0.39      0.26

  swing is the number a wearer reports as "it pushes forward when I turn".

WHAT THE FILTER COSTS AND BUYS
------------------------------
The lag is visible above as the gap between v2 and v2-no-smoothing on
total error; the jitter line under each arm is what that lag buys. Both
numbers belong in the same report — reporting only the cost is how a
filter gets tuned to nothing, and reporting only the benefit is how it
gets tuned until the frame swims. Note that this protocol is a
deliberately fast sweep (35 degrees in a third of a second); ordinary
browsing is slower and the lag is correspondingly smaller.
