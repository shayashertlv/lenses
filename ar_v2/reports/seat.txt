  descent    how far BELOW the nominal placement the frame settles.
             Nominal is v1's answer: pads hung on the bridge landmark.
             A wide-padded frame on a narrow nose settles further.
  seat err   RMS spread of the pad samples' distance to the skin. Zero
             is a pad bedded flush; large is a pad touching on one edge,
             which is what a wearer feels as a pressure point.
  pad load   share of the frame's weight carried by the nose rather
             than the ears.

  A control passes by being BAD: a seat solved against the wrong nose
  should put a pad in the air or bury it in the skin when it meets the
  real one. If every row looks like the baseline, the seat is not
  reading the scan and the scan was not worth taking.

  "nominal placement" is the row to read first: it is a frame hung off
  the bridge landmark, which is what v1 shipped.
