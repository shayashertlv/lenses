  A control passes by being BAD: a seat solved against the wrong nose
  should put a pad in the air or bury it in the skin when it meets the
  real one. If every row looks like the baseline, the seat is not
  reading the scan and the scan was not worth taking.

  "nominal placement" is the row to read first: a frame hung off the
  bridge landmark with no contact solve at all, which is what v1 shipped.
  It uses landmarkHungPose, NOT the initialisation the solver itself
  starts from — those are two different jobs, and sharing one function
  made this control meaningless for a while (docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md Q12).
