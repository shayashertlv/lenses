## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Authored, not assembled. The "optical counter" conceit is carried with conviction: the G-15 field green is literally the lens colour of the 50 Ray-Bans in the catalogue; `FRAME_GLYPHS` silhouettes map 1:1 onto the product tag taxonomy and double as decode placeholders; the capabilities are a numbered directory with honest per-row durations instead of four icon cards; the landing's dispensing docket narrates the 30s wait with the visitor's real face profile, the three real optician paragraphs, and a real frame named with brand and ILS price before its render exists. No competitor could wear this skin without re-tagging a catalogue.

But specificity thins exactly where the argument matters most: free-search's wait rotates canned eyewear trivia and lens-recolor's rotates self-praise, inverting the product's own "show the work, don't assert it" principle that the landing honours. The privacy answer exists at one of four upload points, as an 11px caption. Strongly specific shell, partially generic organs.

## Overall Impression

This is a design with a real point of view executed with unusual discipline — the colour governance survives measurement, the wait-as-content docket is the best latency design I've seen in this category, and the demo never fakes commerce it doesn't have. The single biggest opportunity: the product's stated gating question (what happens to the face photo) is answered at one of four upload points, in the smallest type the system allows, with copy that references a shop that doesn't exist on the standalone pages. For a pharmacy-group audience, that one fix is worth more than everything else combined.

## What's Working

1. **The dispensing docket (landing).** Converts the defining liability (20–50s latency) into the best proof-of-quality moment, using only data the API already returns — the face profile, the three insight paragraphs PRODUCT.md called "the single largest unused asset," and the named frame with a real price before its render exists. Design doing the pitch's work.
2. **The facet system's honesty mechanics (free-search).** Unreachable options dim to a measured 4.8:1 with dashed borders instead of hiding; live "N of M" counts per band; ~70 colour aliases normalised client-side to mirror the backend. The form physically demonstrates the shared-vocabulary moat.
3. **Colour governance that survives contact with the code.** One field green, signal orange genuinely rationed, every muted value's documented ratio independently re-measured and confirmed (ink-3 on card 4.9:1, signal-ink on signal 5.4:1).

## Priority Issues

1. **[P0] The data-posture answer is missing at 3 of 4 upload points.** Only the landing tray carries the privacy line; free-search upload, recolor upload, and the storefront try-on modal say nothing. The storefront modal is precisely what an evaluator's IT/compliance reviewer will screenshot; PRODUCT.md principle 5 requires the answer *where the upload happens*. And the one existing line says "the shop you are browsing stays the data controller" — on the standalone demo that shop doesn't exist, and it never mentions inference runs on the client's own Google Gemini key. **Fix:** a one-line posture element under every upload affordance (consider making it a first-class design-system component, as standard as the price block), reworded for the demo context. **Command:** /impeccable clarify.
2. **[P1] Modal and keyboard accessibility is broken for AT users.** Storefront modal opens without moving focus, no `role="dialog"`/`aria-modal`, ignores Escape, unnamed icon-only close button; photo-tips overlay hides via `opacity:0` so its invisible "Got it" stays in the tab order on every page; free-search and recolor loading views have no live region (20–40s of silence for screen readers, against PRODUCT.md's explicit requirement); facet headings read as "Gender3"/"Shape10" (injected count spans), no fieldset/legend on 102 radios; free-search and recolor lack h1/landmarks. **Fix:** display:none or `inert` the hidden overlay, focus-trap + Escape + labels on modals, aria-live on both waits, fieldset/legend, heading/landmark pass. **Command:** /impeccable harden.
3. **[P2] The wait is content on one surface and filler on two.** Free-search's 20–40s shows generic trivia — including "The right pair of glasses can make you look up to 5 years younger" (free-search.js:23), an unverifiable claim in a product whose spec forbids implied stats — while lens-recolor's wait praises itself ("photorealistic precision"). The landing proved narration works. **Fix:** delete the claim line immediately; narrate free-search's wait with its real material (chosen facets, pool counts as the five filter stages run, matched frame named pre-render); recolor names each colour as it starts. **Command:** /impeccable delight.
4. **[P2] Copy-system drift across surfaces.** US/UK spelling collides on a single page ("Choose 3 Lens Colors" / "3 colours"); "Got It" vs "Got it"; `fmtPrice` renders "799 ₪" while every surface renders "₪799"; "Find my glasses" vs "Find My Match"; storefront sets 76- and 51-char paragraphs in all-caps. For a white-label licensable product, copy discipline is product quality. **Fix:** pick one English, sentence case, one price format, reconcile or rename the two Free Search contracts, drop uppercase from paragraph-length text. **Command:** /impeccable clarify.
5. **[P2] Silent interaction feedback gaps.** The 4th recolor colour is swallowed with no counter flash or explanation (reads as "demo is broken" in a meeting); the Advanced Options caret never flips while 52 controls appear; the disabled-submit hint sits ~4,000px from the photo tray it references and isn't clickable; photo-tips reappears on every upload click including "Change photo". **Fix:** feedback on the cap, rotate the caret, make the hint scroll-to, remember tips-seen per session. **Command:** /impeccable polish.

- `ProgressCreep` mutates `width` per frame on free-search/recolor while landing/storefront use `transform: scaleX` — dodges the contract test (it's JS, not a transition) but violates the motion system's intent.
- `.hero-glow` div in lens-recolor.html has no CSS rule anywhere — dead markup.
- Empty `style=""` attributes on `<strong>` tags in lens-recolor.html — scripted-edit residue.
- 4 gradient stops (#888/#999) absent from DESIGN.md's documented product palette — add them so the detector stays clean.
- Landing docket is always "Docket 01"; storefront tickets number properly.
- Storefront overlay's Material group has 8 options vs 19 standalone — undocumented triage, two different pools.
- `results.css` loads at different cascade positions on landing vs free-search — holds today, fragile.
- Landing CTA `text-overflow` (65px) from the in-page detector is unconfirmed (pane wasn't rendering); worth one visual check in a real window.
- Mobile 375px: zero horizontal overflow on landing and free-search, targets ≥60px — genuinely well-behaved.
- Zero console errors or warnings on all four routes.
