THESIS: Every complaint traces to the same structural fact: quantities that are constants of THIS face — per-vertex nose shape, bridge depth, resting standoff and height — are re-derived every frame from single-frame measurements and then smoothed just enough to smear the noise instead of removing it. The design adds one new object, a PersonModel: a robust, confidence-weighted, pose-diverse accumulator of face-space constants that starts at the canonical prior, only moves on evidence, and provably tightens as 1/sqrt(N). Everything else falls out of consuming converged state: the occluder's per-frame view-locked deform becomes a thin residual on top of the person layer (so the mesh still covers this exact image, but its noise amplitude collapses as the constant part migrates into the model); the depth-borrowing baseline in carryLandmarks/measureAnchors becomes the person mesh instead of the canonical one (so bridge depth and nose depth stop depending on the inert r2 gate — at zero fit trust you get THIS person's converged depth, not the average head's); the seat becomes a two-sided wedge equilibrium solved event-wise against the converged surface with only a bounded, deadbanded per-frame contact residual; and the jitter leaks are closed at their sources (signed rotation rate, noise-floor subtraction, face-space bridge filter, continuous pose-trust weights replacing the binary gate, honest recovery dt, effect-based rebuild deadband). Nothing violates the frame lock, the no-scan-phase rule, or the single-surface invariant: the person layer is read one frame stale like the depth fit already is, frame one is bit-identical to today, and the seat field is still rasterised from the very triangles that write the depth buffer.

=== PERSON MODEL ===
NEW FILE ar/src/person.js — createPersonModel(face) returning a PersonModel. n = face.vertexCount = 468.

DATA MODEL (exact fields; ~19 kB total):
- mu: Float32Array(3n) — fused offset from canonical restBase, face-space cm. Starts 0 (= canonical prior).
- muUsed: Float32Array(3n) — the one-frame-stale read copy consumed by the render/seat path (copied from mu at end of update; keeps frame-one exact and decouples read from write).
- conf: Float32Array(n) — evidence mass per vertex, 0..CONF_CAP (300), with C0 = 1.0 pseudo-mass representing the canonical prior baked into the gain law.
- scale: Float32Array(n) — running mean-absolute innovation (robust sigma proxy), cm, floored at SCALE_FLOOR = 0.05.
- coverNeg/coverPos: Float32Array(n) — evidence mass gathered at signed pose yaw < −0.09 / > +0.09 rad (pose-diversity record; drives readouts and the parallax gate).
- pSxx, pSxy: Float32Array(n) — parallax regression accumulators, touched only for noseSet indices.
- noseSet: Uint16Array(~98) — vertices in the nose box (|x|<2.2, y in [bridge.y−2.5, bridge.y+1.5], z>2.5 on restBase), precomputed at creation. padSet: Uint16Array(~20) — the pad strip (|x| 0.5..1.7, y bridge.y−1.5..+0.3) for wedgeConfidence().
- eNoseRing: Float32Array(15) + cursor — ring of per-frame nose-window RMS residuals for the median trust gate.
- trust: {w, b} — temporally conditioned depth-fit weight and offset (EMA state, TAU_TRUST = 0.3 s).
- frames, resets, lastResetCause.

UPDATE LAW (PersonModel.update({observed, restBase, facingVis, wPose, yawSigned, trustW, dt}), called from occluder.js measureShape after carryLandmarks fills `observed`; facingVis[i] is the facing×visibility gain the loop already computes at occluder.js:798-807):
Per vertex i, with target t = clamp(observed − restBase, ±OFFSET_LIMIT) per component (the clamp already exists):
  w = facingVis[i] · max(0.2, wPose)            // shape channel never fully closes: yaw frames carry the parallax signal
  r = t − mu[i] (per component); r_c ← clamp(r_c, ±HUBER_K·max(scale[i], SCALE_FLOOR)), HUBER_K = 3
  g = w / (C0 + conf[i] + w)                     // gain decays as 1/N: the surface analogue of the 31-sample median window
  mu.x += g·r_x ; mu.y += g·r_y                  // x/y are true ray-pinned measurements
  z-CHANNEL, anti-circularity: mu.z += g·trustW·r_z where trustW is the conditioned depth-fit trust (below). Because the borrowed baseline is rest+muUsed (see INTEGRATION), an observation taken at trust 0 has r_z ≈ 0 by construction — the z channel neither learns nor self-confirms without independent depth evidence. When trust is high, z learns from the camera-slope fit exactly as x/y learn from rays.
  conf[i] = min(conf[i]·λ_f + w, CONF_CAP) with λ_f = 1 − dt/TAU_FORGET (60 s) — slow forgetting bounds staleness.
  scale[i] += g·(mean|r| − scale[i]), floored.
  cover bin += w by sign of yawSigned.
PARALLAX (noseSet only; the one absolute depth measurement a mono camera has): a stored depth error δz leaks into the face-space x innovation as ≈ −δz·sin(yaw) with side-dependent sign. Accumulate BEFORE the mu update: pSxy[i] = pSxy[i]·λ_f + w·sin(yawSigned)·r_x ; pSxx[i] = pSxx[i]·λ_f + w·sin²(yawSigned). Once pSxx[i] > PARALLAX_MIN_SXX (0.02 ≈ 20 effective frames at 18° yaw), apply at ~1 Hz cadence: δz = −pSxy/pSxx; mu.z += APPLY_GAIN(0.3)·δz; pSxy *= (1−APPLY_GAIN). The SIGN is verified by a synthetic-truth harness check (inject +4 mm protrusion error, both yaw signs) before the apply gain is enabled — this is the class of bug the edge-snap sign error was.
CONVERGENCE: with per-frame noise σ and effective mass N, var(mu) = σ²/N; at CONF_CAP the floor is σ/17 ≈ 0.01–0.03 mm — effectively frozen — while λ_f keeps a ~minute-scale tracking bandwidth. NEVER BELOW CANONICAL: mu starts at the prior, moves only under observability weight, innovations are Huber-clamped at 3×MAD, components clamped at OFFSET_LIMIT, and pure noise input walks mu O(σ·sqrt(g)) < 0.5 mm (harness-pinned). Unobserved vertices simply keep the prior.

LAYERING WITH THE VIEW-LOCKED DEFORM (occluder.js measureShape rework): the carried state splits into person + residual. Per frame, per vertex: residual chases (t − muUsed[i]) with EXACTLY today's law — rate = alpha(SHAPE_TAU)·facingVis, first sample adopted whole×facing — and the composite written into the existing `offsets` array is offsets[i] = muUsed[i] + residual[i], so rebuildSurface, the field, the relief, and surfaceOf are untouched (single-surface invariant intact). The residual carries what is genuinely view-dependent (depth-error projection, expression) so the mesh still covers THIS image every frame. Held vertices: when facingVis ≈ 0, today the residual freezes; new — it decays toward 0 at TAU_RESID_DECAY = 2 s scaled by min(1, conf[i]/CONF_HOLD=30), so converged vertices relax to the person shape at hard poses (the far sidewall becomes YOUR nose, not a stale view or the canonical), while a fresh session keeps today's hold semantics exactly.
FRAME-ONE EQUIVALENCE: muUsed is read one frame stale and starts at 0, and the residual law is today's law — frame one's surface is bit-identical to current behaviour. Refinement then converges silently during natural use; no prompts, no phases.

DEPTH TRUST WITHOUT r2 (replaces the inert 0.88–0.92 band): inside fitLandmarkDepth's existing loop (occluder.js:579-591), keep the camera-derived slope, but (a) compute the canonical-depth term c_i from rest+muUsed instead of face.positions, and (b) accumulate a second set of sums restricted to noseSet, yielding e_nose = sqrt(Σ_nose (a·z_i + b − c_i)²/n_nose). The residual is the right statistic where r2 is not: the rigid rotation ramp is EXPLAINED by the affine and cancels out of the residual, while it inflates r2's variance denominator — so e_nose degrades with hallucination, not with pose. Raw trust w_raw = 1 − smoothstep(E_GOOD=0.15, E_BAD=0.5 cm, e_nose_median15) using the eNoseRing median; conditioned trust.w += (w_raw − trust.w)·(1−exp(−dt/TAU_TRUST)), and trust.b EMA'd the same way. carryLandmarks and measureAnchors consume trust.w/trust.b in place of fit.weight/fit.b (occluder still stores raw fit for __ar). Consequences: the bridge pin's depth at trust 0 is the PERSON's converged bridge depth (rest+mu at vertex 6), not the canonical average — killing empirics seat-cause-3 and most of the 6.8 mm cross-pose bridge wander, since that wander is borrowed-depth error rotating into x through the ray walk. The 0.04-band dither dies twice over: residual statistic plus 0.3 s time constant (weight sd under noise: 0.19 → <0.02).

IDENTITY CHANGE: person.reset('identity') is called wherever resetFit fires (frame.js isDifferentFace path) and from remeasure(). Tripwire for swaps the scalars miss: compute e_nose against BOTH baselines (person and canonical — 98 extra mults); if median-15 person-residual exceeds canonical-residual by 50% for 2 s continuously, reset('regression'). Deform-toggle off resets as today.

INTEGRATION POINTS: occluder.js createOccluder stores data.person = createPersonModel(face); measureShape calls person.update and composes offsets; fitLandmarkDepth gains basePositions param; anchors.js carryLandmarks/measureAnchors gain basePositions (rest+muUsed) for depth borrowing with face.positions fallback (harness bare-state callers unchanged); frame.js passes state.occluder.userData.person's positions into measureAnchors the same one-frame-stale way it already passes depthFit (frame.js:243). Per-frame cost: one extra O(468) pass plus O(98) sums — ~0.05 ms.

=== SEAT SOLVE ===
GOAL: replace optical-height-then-one-scalar-push with a two-sided wedge equilibrium — DOFs: descent h along bridgeUp (cm of vertical), standoff s along face +Z, optional roll φ about +Z (flag-gated stage). Solved event-wise against the converged surface; per-frame work is a bounded residual. All contact arithmetic stays in seat()'s bridge-relative frame (nose.js:247-257), preserving the exact bridge-error cancellation.

MODEL SIDE (fit.js analyseModel, load time): findBridgeSurface keeps its binning; additionally split the bins by side of the centreline into contactSides = {left, right, centre} (centre band |x−centre.x| < 0.15·halfWidth is the saddle set, counted for contact but not for bearing balance). Derive padSeparationM = mean |x| gap between the rearmost 20% of left and right bins ×2, and padY = depth-weighted centroid y of those bins. Harness already asserts spread across the centreline (pipeline-check.js:507-526) — now it's load-bearing.

WEDGE QUERIES on the existing field (nose.js, pure derived reductions on the same Float32Array):
- field.normalAt(x,y): n ∝ normalize(−∂z/∂x, −∂z/∂y, 1), central differences over ±CELL (0.1 cm) via depthAt; NaN-propagating.
- field.widthAt(y, zRef): from the ridge column outward per side, find the first x where depthAt(x,y) crosses zRef (field falls monotonically off the ridge at pad heights); linear-interp the crossing; return xR+xL span, NaN if no crossing inside WINDOW or any EMPTY cell.
- field.wedgeSlope(y0,y1,zRef) = (widthAt(y1)−widthAt(y0))/(y1−y0) — canonical value ≈ 0.054 cm width per cm descent; plausibility band [0.02, 0.12].

EQUILIBRIUM (nose.js seatEquilibrium({surface, contactSides, toFace, anchors, bridgeUp, lensBox, pupilTarget, wedgeConf, limits})):
Descent slide v(h) = −(h/max(bridgeUp.y,0.2))·bridgeUp (reuses the pupil-slide scaling so h is vertical cm; h=0 is today's optical height, +h is down the nose). For contact j at placed p_j: I_j(h,s) = (skin(x_j, y_j+v_y) + shiftZ) − (p_j.z + v_z + s), skin via depthAt in the bridge-relative frame exactly as today.
CONTACT-PATCH SOFT REDUCTION (replaces the flip-prone argmax): per side, I_side = Σ λ_j·I_j / Σ λ_j with λ_j = exp((I_j − max_j I_j)/τ_c), τ_c = 0.02 cm — continuous in every input, concentrated within 0.2 mm of the max, so it cannot step when the resting bin flips. Compensate its ≤τ_c downward bias with PAD_SINK′ = PAD_SINK − τ_c/2.
Conditions: (1) contact — s(h) = max(I_L, I_R, I_C)|s=0 − PAD_SINK′ (rearmost patch rests at sink depth); (2) bearing — d(h) = PAD_SINK′ − min(I_L, I_R)|s=s(h) is the second side's float; (3) optics as SOFT cost — e_p(h) = (achievedPupilFraction(h) − pupilTarget)·lensHeight. Minimise J(h) = d(h)² + e_p(h)²/W_BEAR over h ∈ [−0.3, H_MAX=1.2], W_BEAR = 16 (1 mm of pad float trades against 4 mm of pupil error). Solve by coarse scan at 0.1 cm (16 evaluations × ~810 bilinear lookups ≈ 13k — ~0.1 ms) plus one parabolic refinement. Physio sanity: the canonical wedge gives dh/dΔpad ≈ 1.9 mm/mm, which the resting-height measurable pins. Roll (flag): φ = clamp(atan(residual per-side z-gap / pad x-span), ±3°) about +Z through the bridge contact, then recompute s once; ships only after the stability measurable passes with it on.
CONFIDENCE BLEND: wedgeConf = min(1, meanConf(padSet)/CONF_WEDGE=60) from the person model. Applied descent h_applied = wedgeConf·h*. Frame one and gate-starved sessions have wedgeConf = 0 → exactly today's behaviour (optical height + worst-contact push); as the pad strip converges the equilibrium takes over continuously. This is also the degeneracy answer: flat noses (widthAt NaN, slope out of band), occluded sidewalls (<10 valid contacts on a side), canonical-anchored sessions — all force mode='fallback' (h=0, today's seat), reported in __ar.seat.mode.

TIMESCALES (the jitter fix): (h*, s*, φ*) are face constants — the coarse solve runs only on events: person-layer rebuild causes, commitFit deadband crossings, fit-settings/model changes, remeasure, plus a 2 s safety cadence. frame.js holds and eases them: h through TAU_H = 0.4 s, s through the existing SEAT_TAU = 0.15 s pattern with its eased-minus-raw compensation, both cleared by remeasure() exactly as state.seatPush is today. PER FRAME, a single contact pass at the held (h̄,s̄) computes worstNow; correction c = clamp(worstNow − PAD_SINK′, ±RESID_MAX=0.15) passes a 0.03 cm deadband then the SEAT_TAU ease — guaranteeing non-penetration within deadband+ease every frame while the equilibrium never chases field noise.

INTEGRATION (fit.js solvePlacement staged order preserved): scale → orientation → position-hang unchanged. Stage 4 becomes: compute the optical vertical correction exactly as today (this defines h=0), then if resolve flag set call seatEquilibrium, else use seatState.h̄; apply position.addScaledVector(bridgeUp, −h_applied/max(up.y,0.2)); stage 5 standoff: position.z += s (equilibrium s or per-frame-corrected held s); offsetZ after, as today. solvePlacement gains an optional seatState param {h, s, resolve} supplied by frame.js (which owns all easing state, keeping solvePlacement effectively stateless per call). pupilHeightInLens is unchanged as a function but demoted from constraint to VERDICT: reported per frame, with widthVerdict-style bands ('sits low' below 0.62, 'sits high' above 0.28), because where the frame rests on THIS nose is now an output.

STABILITY ARGUMENT: the field the equilibrium sees is rest+muUsed+residual where the constant part has 1/N noise; the solve runs on events against it; per-frame action is deadbanded and bounded; the soft reduction removes the argmax discontinuity; and the seat measurable (4) pins push RMS < 0.2 mm under ±0.3 mm injected landmark noise at 0/20/30° and the diag pillow pose.

=== RISKS ===
  - Self-confirmation: borrowed baseline = person mesh feeds the model its own output and mu drifts to a confident wrong shape -> z-channel updates only under depth-fit trust or parallax (at trust 0 the z innovation is ~0 by construction — no learning, no reinforcement); Huber clamps; never-below-canonical harness check; residual-vs-canonical tripwire resets the model if it fits worse than the prior for 2 s
  - Parallax sign/geometry error (the edge-snap-class bug) corrupts nose depth confidently -> APPLY_GAIN ships 0 in stage 3; enabled only after the synthetic-truth sign check passes at both yaw signs; corrections incremental (0.3 gain) and drained from the accumulator so a wrong step self-erases
  - Weighted median subtly shifts widths/scale on real faces vs today's estimator -> stage-2 equivalence check (frontal streams identical < 0.1 mm); FIT_DEADBAND and IDENTITY_TOLERANCE untouched; diag replay pins noseWidthRatio in 0.9–1.1
  - Equilibrium descent and standoff fight (coupled-solve oscillation on a noisy field) -> coarse solve event-driven against the converged layer; s eliminated inside the solve (s(h) closed-form); per-frame authority limited to a deadbanded ±1.5 mm residual; TAU_H 0.4 s; seat-stability measurable is the gate
  - Noise-floor tracker freezes wrong and eats slow real motion — a new lag class -> floor updates only below 3× current floor with a 10%/s rise leak; 2-sigma margin; lag harness re-pins the sweep within 5%; smoother 'bounded' readout would expose divergence live
  - Contact-patch soft reduction under-pushes and a pad clips the drawn surface -> PAD_SINK' compensation keeps the relief inequality slack; the pinned no-frame-inside-head harness block runs on every stage; tau_c is 0.2 mm against a 1.8 mm relief
  - Crossfade payload lets degraded hard-pose measurements leak into eyeLineY/ears (weights too generous) -> wLive → 0 beyond the upper ramp edges reproduces today's carried behaviour at extremes; wLive is EMA'd at 0.3 s; diag replay checks anchor sanity on the worst captures
  - Refactor of measureShape/solvePlacement breaks frame-one or harness-bare-state callers -> basePositions/seatState are optional params with exact-fallback paths; stage-3 frame-one bit-equality check; wedgeConf=0 forced-fallback bit-equality check in stage 4
  - Perf regression: extra O(468) pass, 16-eval equilibrium, widthAt scans -> equilibrium is event-driven (~0.1 ms when it runs); per-frame additions ≈ 0.05 ms; harness timing assertion: updateFrame added cost < 0.5 ms typical frame, < 1.5 ms on solve events
  - Person model converges to a view-biased shape because the user's regime is one-sided (always pillow-left) -> coverNeg/coverPos record per-vertex pose diversity; wedgeConf and parallax gates require mass, not merely frames; one-sided sessions simply keep more weight on the prior for the unseen side — degrading to today, never below it
