# glasses3d full code review (2026-10-18)

Owner request (2026-10-18): "please review glasses3d thoroughly. no reason to investigate evidence yourself, just review
everything, the most important is the code, so be extremely thorough with the code."

Method: the 776 tracked files at `31daa31` (about 98k lines of source, 95k lines of tests, 29k lines of plan documents)
were split into 20 disjoint slices plus one end-to-end integration slice. Each reviewer read every line of its slice,
grepped callers, read the covering tests, and confirmed suspected defects by re-reading the code and, where cheap, with
a small reproducer (no `data/` evidence read, no paid calls, no repository changes). The coordinator re-checked every P1
and the main cross-slice P2s in the code. The full suites were run. Detailed reports (every finding with file:line,
failure scenario, evidence and fix direction), the reproducer scripts and the suite logs are local and git-ignored:
`data/review_20261018/{reports,reproducers,suites}/`. Finding IDs below (e.g. `OE-01`) refer to those reports.

---

## 1. Verdict

- **The assembled automation is real and its core holds.** No reviewer found a path that sends a request without a
  consumed approval, starts a request at or after the settled stop, reserves past the cap, settles twice or loses a
  hold on a crash (`agent/budget.py`, `pricing.py`). No sandbox escape was found in `g3d/safe_mode.py` + `worker.py` or
  in any facade helper. The same export is validated, captured, delivered and served for review. The exporter, the
  spec validator, the camera math (Blender and arview conversions re-derived exactly), the float64 temple builder and
  the arview renderer are careful, heavily tested code.
- **Of the 25 ranked defects of 2026-10-13, 19 are fixed, 5 are partly fixed and 1 is open** (section 3).
- **The new defects sit where work was added after M5**: failure and recovery paths of paid runs, owner edit mode
  (M6-M10), the per-photo arm-opening fit (M5-fix-5), and generality of the print and seating tools for new products.
  Six P1s (section 2), about 100 P2s after merging duplicates (section 4), about 200 P3s (in the reports).
- **Tests are green apart from known environment drift** (section 5): Python 4,484 tests, all pass alone except the
  stale golden render; arview 507/507, qa 106/106, `tsc` clean. But several important paths are untested, two proofs
  can no longer show what they claim (WP6 T5 rehearsal, the desktop test's money terms), and the suite is still
  run-location and load sensitive.

## 4. P2 findings, grouped (duplicates across slices merged; IDs point to the reports)

### A. Paid runs: money, loop, finish, resume
- **Trusted worker results are not sanitised, and one NaN ends a paid run.** `g3d/worker.py:409-418` returns `g3d_call` and
  read results without `rpc.to_jsonable`; `rpc.encode` is strict; the host treats a `FrameError` reply as fatal by
  design (`agent/journal.py:434`). Reproduced: `hide_eyewire(ramp_mm=0)` gives NaN lift (`g3d/seat/plan.py:821-830`,
  `calls.py:69` checks only finiteness). A NaN vertex left by a snippet does the same through `bounds`. (SEAT-4, SS-6)
- **The finish runway ignores the settled stop** (`agent/finish_runway.py:73-83`) and is off in settled-only mode
  (`loop.py:913-920`), yet the instructions still say "the finish reserve allows another fix". Under the owner's
  current terms it never warns; Prada run 3's draft failure can recur from the stop instead of the reserve. (ML-2, JP-7)
- **Resume:** the approved estimate is always a fresh conversation, but `--resume` replays the whole history (JP-4,
  E2E-09); `best_checkpoint` survives across runs, so a resumed unfinished run re-delivers the older run's model
  (`job_scene.py:101, 116`, `delivery.py:605`; JP-5, E2E-03); resume starts at the head while the owner's notes refer
  to the draft's checkpoint (E2E-04); a `job stop` written while the detached runner starts is deleted as stale
  (`loop.py:1751-1753`; E2E-08).
- **Tool timeouts below their own worst case** (a timed-out tool keeps the scene lock, so every following tool call is
  a paid `E_BUSY` turn): `compare_look_to_photos` 960 s worst case vs 760 s (the side-light capture of `d39f954` was
  never counted, `loop.py:155`), `edit_report` 1,800 vs 1,690 s, `arview_capture` 410 vs 310 s. (AT-04, AT-14, E2E-11)
- **The over-length summariser can drop the keys Astra needs** (`agent/tool_reports.py:125-133`, 12 top-level keys):
  for the first `finish` it can drop `review` (`required_refs`, `review_call_id`) that the second call must echo, and
  `surface_quality` from look comparisons. (JP-3, LIKELY)
- **Temple stage failure handling:** when the worker cannot restart, `_apply`'s rollback raises `AttributeError` and
  hides the fatal `WorkerFailed`; the paid child keeps running (`lab.py:1375-1381`, reproduced); facet tools treat only
  `WorkerFailed` as fatal, so `CheckpointMismatch` reaches Astra as text (`temple_lab/tools.py:585, 640-648`). (TLC-1/2)
- **Stage money is all-or-nothing:** the specialist is told the reserve remainder, not what is left before the settled
  stop; a child request reaching the stop ends the whole job with the child's accepted temples unapplied and no money
  left for the parent's finish (`tools/temple.py:48-61`, `stage.py:294-300`). (TLC-3, E2E-10)

### B. Session and recovery (`agent/seeing.py`, `journal.py`, `job_scene.py`)
- A rollback journaled under a derived call id is replayed on resume and **restores the refused edit** (SS-1, reproduced).
- An export taken while the scene had drifted is cached under the head's fingerprint and served from then on (SS-2,
  reproduced); the export cache key has no exporter identity (SS-7).
- A `WorkerError` during a rollback restore escapes with no journal entry, leaving the scene changed (SS-3, reproduced);
  the owner-edit scope check swallows fatal errors and still reports "rolled back" (SS-5).
- A refit of some photos becomes the default basis, so finish reviews fewer owner photos (SS-4) and the print refuses
  the subset basis with an unexplained error (PP1-05).

### C. Photos, sides and intake
- **EXIF orientation:** intake keeps the tag and sends raw bytes to Astra, while every tool works in the
  EXIF-transposed frame (`agent/jobs.py:100-110, 262-272`); reproduced with Orientation=6. If the provider does not
  apply EXIF, Astra's pixel picks land in the wrong place. Only photos over 20 MiB get an upright copy today.
- **"left" means opposite things.** `g3d_host/spec.py:607, 689`, arview reports, `__crop_left` files and worn-view
  `side` fields call x < 0 "left" (the wearer's right); the brief Astra reads says side labels name the wearer's side
  (`agent/brief.py:27`) and `cameras.py:10` uses the wearer's convention. Nothing tells Astra; it can edit the wrong arm.
  (DOC-7, AQ-3; the Oakley labels already needed `cdb52a2`)
- The temple stage sends the original photo bytes, not the job's transport copies: photos over 20 MiB end the parent
  run `failed`, `.gif` raises a KeyError (`temple_lab/package.py:108-116`, `cli.py:771-781`). (E2E-07)
- `read_image` refuses `cameras/`, where the camera-fit overlays live, although the description says native panels are
  readable. (JP-6)

### D. Owner edit mode (M6-M10)
- **Amounts not shown for moves:** a move-only understanding needs no preview, so the paid EDIT round applies an amount
  Astra chose, the M10 failure (`agent/tools/owner_edit.py:71-90`). Previews pick "revealing" views by exact label only
  (`owner_preview.py:43-52`: misses `left`, `back`, `Top`, `rear three quarter`, rotations). (AT-02, AT-05)
- **Normals:** the transport frame comes from the first fan triangle, so authored normals rotate up to 180 deg under a
  pure translation on sliver-led polygons (`owner_surface.py:145-163`, 471/2,000 > 1 deg; OE-02); batched and sequential
  application give different meshes (OE-03); `consistent_normals` on a correct mesh marks every edge sharp (OE-04);
  mirrored objects are refused or turned inside out (OE-05); modifiers that override custom normals make a verified
  repair invisible (OE-09, PLAUSIBLE).
- Exact geometric tests on float32 world data: decimation removes nothing and fillets refuse on any tilted geometry
  (OE-06); `material_adjust` refuses open lenses ("lens a bit darker" fails, OE-07); the thickness proof costs about
  107 s at 62k triangles, run twice per operation inside a 180 s apply deadline (OE-10).
- **`edit-apply`** records the round before validating, so a failed or stale apply wedges a paid understanding (OE-08);
  its result is not a delivery (no AR, no seating/print gate; `job review` / `job verdict` cannot use it), so the owner's
  "3mm looks good" acceptance sits only in a hand-made file (E2E-12, DOC-5).
- Owner-edit rounds of a printed product can never finish: print evidence is not copied into the round, so `finish`
  returns `E_PRINT` (`edits.py:184-193`, `owner_stage.py:51-54`; E2E-05).
- Windows MAX_PATH: `LongPathsEnabled` is 0; the nested edit specialist's path is 248 characters with a 20-character job
  id and passes 260 at 33 (ids may be 69, and each edit generation adds about 64). (E2E-06)
- Image wall in edit mode: photo/before/after strips are three photos wide and get shrunk by the provider (x0.26 for
  4032 px photos) while labelled "native"; close-ups and map tiles are sent uncomposed (AT-06); the model map makes a
  target per polygon/edge/vertex on smooth meshes, over 600 s per photo at 50k edges (AT-07).

### E. What Astra is told vs what the code does
- The look pair is downscaled when the crop is wider than about 1,600 px, while Astra is told "full-size, unchanged
  pixels, never shrunk" (`look_panels.py:233-252`, pinned by `test_look_panels.py:110-120`; M9 item 1). (AT-01)
- `look.verified_report` reads the arview report as cp1252 (`agent/tools/look.py:92`, the only runtime text read
  without an encoding); names containing e.g. a closing curly quote raise `UnicodeDecodeError`, which blocks every look
  comparison and therefore every `finish`. One-line fix. (AT-03)
- Sandbox refusal hints send Astra to `load_font(family)` and `deliver()`, which are not Astra tools
  (`g3d/safe_mode.py:76-77`); three paid refusals in Prada run 6. (DOC note)
- Sandbox hardening: `'{0.gi_frame}'.format(gen)` and other non-underscore attributes pass the static `.format` rule and
  the runtime `getattr` guard; only the audit hook stops frame access, leaving a read-only attribute-to-string leak. Fix:
  run `_name_rule` on each format field (`safe_mode.py:596-608`). (WE-1)
- Spec generality: the orientation heuristic (#12, AT-08) and frames narrower than 95 mm failing "units" (AT-09).

### F. Cameras and comparisons (`g3d_host/`)
- Arm-opening fit: the 15 deg grid misses thin arms and an unchanged angle is still labelled observed (CC-02); arm
  visibility is judged on the model, not the photo (CC-03); `compare_to_photos` never applies the fitted openings, so
  its metrics show authored arms at cameras fitted with opened ones (`compare.py:546-573`, `seeing.py:711-738`; CC-04).
- Mattes: glossy-floor leaves clear-lens holes open, so the automatic choice can never pick it for clear-lens full-rim
  frames (CC-05); rimless frames never get the automatic floor matte (CC-06); compare reports raw IoU for rimless
  (CC-07); cut-out PNG alpha is dropped without compositing (CC-11).
- Near-orthographic fitted cameras (perspective 1e-9..1e-4) break both conversions: 47 px Blender error at 1e-6
  (`blender_camera.py:98-105`, `ar_camera.py:23-33`; CC-08).
- Product data in runtime modules: the FT1119/Prada table in `fit_views.py:129-165`, `"TOM_FORD"` in `fitmesh.py:28-30`
  (CLI/tests only) (CC-09); matte memory peaks at about 6.4 GB per call at the 40 MP intake limit and pools are not
  sized to free memory (CC-10).

### G. Temple stage and fit
- `offset_chain` with taper 0 ("0 = none") leaks the offset up to the next knot, 1.78 mm 1 mm outside the range, with no
  note (`geometry.py:316-338`; TLG-1, reproduced).
- Readings apply a fixed 0.3 deg opening uncertainty even when the opening was never calibrated, so weak creases can be
  classed "certain" and the owner-approved visible-angle choice is then refused (`readings.py:145, 587-615`; TLG-2).
- A visible-angle declaration is invalidated by any later edit or reading anywhere, contrary to the tool text's "may"
  (TLG-3, TLC-4); it is refused with a false "unrelated reading" reason when the moving face's own corner is typed or
  solved (`fit.py:3135-3161`; TF-2); the fit's position warning still names the design node (10 mm "away" for a corner
  fitted exactly), and a test pins the misleading text (`fit.py:3564-3590`, `test_temple_fit.py:989`; TF-1).
- The visibility gate of the angle choice (`crease_ar.visible_target`) has no unit test (TLG-4).

### H. Temple builder and Blender temple writer
- **The builder still emits float32-degenerate triangles** (band closure at near-perpendicular borders, roll lines near

  not met (`build.py:259-293, 497-510`, `arrange.py:136-147, 507-586`; TB-01). Astra-settable sizes have no lower bound:
  `roll_mm` 0.001 gives 976 flagged triangles, 1e-6 an internal `E_TRIANGULATE` (TB-02).
- `template_from_mesh` fills split or holed bodies instead of refusing (TB-03); `check_round_corners` reports only the
  first corner of an all-smooth section (TB-04); `loft_sections` resampling destroys rounded corners (TB-05);
  `polygon_section` keeps a duplicate closing point and the loft then raises `E_TRIANGULATE` (TB-06).
- Variant renders keep WeightedNormal modifiers that the real write removes, so compared variants are not shaded like
  the applied result (`render.py:799-819`; G3B-02); faceting leaves text/curve and thicker logos behind (G3B-03) and
  collapses decals whose foot point is on a top/bottom face (G3B-04).

## 6. Cross-cutting patterns

1. **Recovery paths are the weak spot.** Most P2s in A and B are on paths that run only after something else failed
   (rollback, restart, resume, timeout, unsendable result). They are reachable in paid runs and are mostly untested.
2. **Features added after M5 were not wired into every consumer.** Per-photo arm openings reach look and photo_read but
   not compare or the print (P1-5, CC-04); the side-light capture changed look cost and time but not the timeout table;
   the composition rule of M9 reached look and finish but not the edit tools; draft delivery covers the statuses known
   at M5-prep but not later ones.
3. **Exact tests on float32 data.** The builder (TB-01), the owner/health ops (OE-02, OE-06) and the transport of
   normals assume exactness that float32 metres cannot hold. One project-wide minimum feature size and relative
   tolerances would close a family of defects.
4. **Text Astra reads drifts from the code.** Look "never shrunk" (AT-01), visible angle "may" (TLG-3), budget_status in
   settled-only mode (ML-5), "left" (section 4C), sandbox hints (4E), `temple_stage` advertised in edit mode (P1-2).
   A contract test per tool description (named fields, refusals, units, side convention) would catch most of these.
5. **Generality for unseen products** is still mostly in print and seating (P1-6, PP1-03/04, SEAT-1/2/3, G3B-01), plus
   cameras for clear-lens and rimless frames (CC-05/06/07).
